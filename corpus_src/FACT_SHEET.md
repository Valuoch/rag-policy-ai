## PTO & leave (HR-002)
- PTO accrual by tenure: 0–2 years of service 15 days/year; 3–5 years 20 days/year; 6+ years 25 days/year. Accrues each pay period (1/24 per period). New hires accrue from day one; may go up to 5 days negative balance with manager approval.
- Carryover: max 5 unused PTO days into the next year; carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they are forfeited.
- Requests in Workday: at least 2 weeks' notice for 3+ consecutive days; at least 48 hours for shorter absences. Manager approves/denies within 3 business days.
- Payout at separation: accrued unused PTO paid out (where state law requires, all of it; otherwise up to 10 days).
- Sick leave: separate bank of 10 days/year, front-loaded Jan 1 (prorated for new hires), no carryover, not paid out. Doctor's note required after 3 consecutive sick days.
- Bereavement: 5 paid days for immediate family; 2 paid days for extended family.
- Jury duty: full pay up to 10 working days per year.
- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for birthing/primary caregiver, 8 weeks for secondary caregiver; eligible after 6 months of employment; must be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption; can be taken in up to 2 blocks.
- Volunteer time off: 1 paid day per year.
- FMLA up to 12 weeks unpaid job-protected leave for eligible employees (12 months' service, 1,250 hours).
- Sabbatical: 4 weeks paid after 7 years of continuous service, scheduled with 3 months' notice.

## Holidays (HR-003)
- 11 paid company holidays in 2026 plus 2 floating holidays (use any time in the calendar year, no carryover; request like PTO).
- 2026 dates: New Year's Day Thu Jan 1; Martin Luther King Jr. Day Mon Jan 19; Presidents' Day Mon Feb 16; Memorial Day Mon May 25; Juneteenth Fri Jun 19; Independence Day observed Fri Jul 3 (Jul 4 is a Saturday); Labor Day Mon Sep 7; Thanksgiving Thu Nov 26; Day after Thanksgiving Fri Nov 27; Christmas Eve Thu Dec 24; Christmas Day Fri Dec 25.
- Holiday falling on Saturday is observed the preceding Friday; on Sunday, the following Monday.
- A holiday that falls during approved PTO is not charged as PTO.
- Non-exempt employees required to work on a holiday receive 1.5x pay plus a substitute day off within 60 days.
- New hires get floating holidays prorated: start before July 1 = 2, on/after July 1 = 1.

## Remote & hybrid (HR-004)
- Three work arrangements: On-site, Hybrid (default for office-assigned roles), Remote.
- Hybrid: in office 3 days/week, with Tuesday and Wednesday as required anchor days plus one more day of choice.
- Fully remote requires VP approval and HR sign-off; reviewed annually.
- Home-office stipend: $500 one-time (equipment, within first 90 days) plus $50/month internet reimbursement (via Concur) for Hybrid and Remote employees.
- Work from another country: up to 20 working days per calendar year, with manager + HR approval requested at least 10 business days in advance; some countries are prohibited (sanctioned countries).
- Working from another US state for more than 30 days requires HR approval (tax/payroll registration).
- Must use company VPN (GlobalProtect) on any network other than the office; public Wi-Fi only via VPN.
- Must be reachable during core hours 10:00–15:00 local time.

## Expenses (FIN-001)
- Submit in SAP Concur within 30 days of incurring the expense; expenses over 90 days old are not reimbursed except with CFO exception.
- Itemized receipts required for any expense of $25 or more.
- Approval: direct manager approves; single expense report over $5,000 also requires VP approval.
- Reimbursement paid within 10 business days of final approval, via payroll direct deposit.
- Meals while traveling (domestic): daily cap $75 (guideline: breakfast $15, lunch $20, dinner $40). International: $100/day.
- Client entertainment: up to $100 per person, must list attendees, company, and business purpose; alcohol reimbursable only for client entertainment, never for solo meals.
- Mileage: current IRS standard mileage rate for personal vehicle business use (rate is published in Concur); commuting is not reimbursable.
- Corporate card (Amex): issued to employees who travel 4+ times per year or by manager request; personal charges forbidden on corporate card; reconcile monthly.
- Non-reimbursable: traffic/parking fines, personal entertainment, seat or class upgrades not pre-approved, minibar, spouse/companion travel, gym fees on trips, lost personal items.
- Gift cards to customers require Legal pre-approval; gifts to customers capped at $100 per person per year.

## Travel (FIN-002)
- Book all air, hotel, and rail through Navan; bookings outside Navan need justification.
- Book flights at least 14 days in advance when possible.
- Class of air: economy for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for flights 6+ hours; business class only for flights over 10 hours AND with VP approval.
- Hotels: up to $250/night standard; $350/night in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, Boston, Seattle, Washington DC, London).
- Rental cars: compact or midsize; decline extra insurance in US (covered by company policy); rideshare/taxi OK.
- Pre-trip approval by manager for all travel; international travel requires additional approval from department VP and registration with the travel security program (International SOS) at least 7 days before departure.
- Per diem rules follow FIN-001 meal caps.
- Loyalty points may be kept by the employee but must not influence booking choices.
- Bleisure (adding personal days): allowed if the added cost is borne by employee.

## Information security (SEC-001)
- Data classification: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted (Restricted = customer data, PII, credentials, financial results before release).
- Passwords: minimum 14 characters; use 1Password (company-provided) for all work passwords; never reuse; no forced periodic rotation unless compromise suspected.
- MFA required for all company systems (Okta Verify push or hardware key; SMS not allowed).
- Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes idle; lock manually when leaving device.
- Full-disk encryption (BitLocker / FileVault) mandatory on all laptops.
- Patching: critical vulnerabilities patched within 72 hours; high within 14 days; others within 30 days.
- Report suspected incidents (including lost/stolen devices and clicked phishing links) within 1 hour to security@lumora.example, #sec-incidents, or the security hotline 1-512-555-0199.
- Security awareness training: on hire (within 30 days) and annually; quarterly phishing simulations.
- USB mass storage blocked by default; exceptions via ServiceNow ticket approved by Security.
- Personal phones may access email/Slack only if enrolled in Microsoft Intune (MDM) app protection.
- Access reviews quarterly; least privilege; access removed within 24 hours of termination (immediately for involuntary).

## Acceptable use (SEC-002)
- Company devices for business; limited incidental personal use OK if it doesn't interfere or violate policy.
- Software only from the Lumora Self Service portal or approved via ServiceNow request; no pirated software.
- Generative AI: only approved tools (Lumora's enterprise AI assistant and GitHub Copilot Business); never put Confidential or Restricted data into unapproved/public AI tools; AI output must be reviewed by a human before use.
- Prohibited: crypto mining, accessing illegal/explicit content, disabling security tools (EDR: CrowdStrike), sharing accounts, running unauthorized network scanners.
- Monitoring: company may monitor company systems; no expectation of privacy on company devices.
- Social media: don't speak on behalf of Lumora unless authorized by Communications; never disclose confidential info.
- Email: no auto-forwarding of company email to personal accounts.

## Privacy (LEG-001)
- Applies GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and other applicable laws.
- Data subject requests (access, deletion, correction) answered within 30 days; forward to privacy@lumora.example within 2 business days of receipt.
- Personal data breaches reported to privacy team within 24 hours of discovery (and to security per SEC-001 within 1 hour); regulators notified within 72 hours where required.
- Retention: customer data deleted within 90 days after contract end; employee HR records kept 7 years after separation; recruiting data of unsuccessful candidates 2 years; financial records 7 years.
- Privacy impact assessment (PIA) required before launching any new processing of personal data or new vendor handling personal data.
- Vendors handling personal data must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
- Privacy training annually.

## Performance & compensation (HR-005)
- Annual review cycle: self-review in December, manager reviews and calibration in January; mid-year check-in in June.
- Rating scale 1–5 (1 Does not meet, 2 Partially meets, 3 Meets, 4 Exceeds, 5 Exceptional).
- Merit increases effective April 1; promotions considered twice a year (April 1 and October 1).
- Annual bonus target as % of base salary: individual contributors levels L1–L3 8%, L4–L5 10%, managers 15%, directors 20%, VPs 30%; paid in March; must be employed on payout date; prorated for hires after Jan 1, no bonus if hired after October 1.
- Performance Improvement Plan (PIP): 60 days, with check-ins every 2 weeks.
- Pay bands reviewed annually; pay transparency: salary ranges included in job postings.
- 1:1s with manager at least every 2 weeks.

## Benefits (HR-006)
- Eligibility: full-time employees (30+ hours/week) from first day of employment; enroll within 30 days of hire; open enrollment in November for following calendar year; qualifying life event changes within 30 days.
- Medical: three plans (PPO, HDHP with HSA, HMO). Lumora pays 90% of employee premium and 75% of dependent premiums.
- HSA employer contribution: $750 individual / $1,500 family per year.
- Dental and vision: Lumora pays 100% of employee premium.
- 401(k): 100% match on first 4% of salary contributed + 50% on next 2% (max 5% employer match); immediate vesting; auto-enrollment at 3%.
- Life insurance: 2x annual base salary, company-paid; AD&D 2x.
- Disability: short-term 60% pay up to 12 weeks, long-term 60% up to $15,000/month, both company-paid.
- Learning & development stipend: $1,500 per calendar year (courses, certifications, conferences, books).
- Wellness stipend: $50/month (gym, fitness apps, etc.) via Concur.
- EAP: free, confidential, 6 counseling sessions per issue per year.
- Commuter benefits: pre-tax transit/parking up to IRS limits.

## Conduct (HR-001, HR-007)
- Report concerns to manager, HR (hr@lumora.example), or anonymous Ethics hotline 1-800-555-0142.
- Investigations begin within 5 business days of a report; target completion within 30 days.
- Strict non-retaliation.
- Gifts: may accept gifts of nominal value up to $100 per year per source; no cash or cash equivalents ever.
- Conflicts of interest: disclose in writing to HR and manager; annual disclosure certification each January.
- Code of Conduct acknowledgment annually.
- Dress code: business casual in office; client-appropriate when meeting customers.
- Relationships: romantic relationships between a manager and a direct or indirect report must be disclosed to HR.
- Workplace violence and weapons are prohibited on company premises.
- Drug & alcohol: alcohol only at approved company events in moderation.

## Onboarding & offboarding (HR-008)
- Offer acceptance → background check (5–7 business days).
- Laptop shipped to arrive at least 2 business days before start date (shipped ~5 business days before).
- Form I-9 Section 1 by first day, documents verified by the 3rd business day.
- Mandatory trainings within first 30 days: security awareness, anti-harassment, privacy, code of conduct.
- 30/60/90-day check-ins with manager; onboarding buddy assigned.
- Introductory period: 90 days.
- Offboarding: manager notifies HR in Workday at least 2 weeks before last day for voluntary resignations (2 weeks' notice requested from employees; 4 weeks for managers and above).
- Return company equipment within 10 business days after last day (prepaid return box provided).
- Access revoked at end of last working day (immediately for involuntary terminations).
- Final paycheck per state law; COBRA info sent within 14 days; exit interview offered.
- Knowledge transfer plan required for departing employees.
