# HR-008 Onboarding & Offboarding Procedures

- Document ID: HR-008
- Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Procedures
- Version: 3.1
- Effective date: 2026-01-01
- Owner: Chief People Officer
- Applies to: All new and departing employees of Lumora Systems, Inc. in the United States, their hiring and departing managers, HR, IT, Security, Finance and Payroll

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out how Lumora Systems, Inc. ("Lumora") welcomes new employees and how it manages employee departures. A consistent onboarding process helps new employees become productive quickly, understand Lumora's policies, and feel part of the team. A consistent offboarding process protects company systems and data, makes sure departing employees are paid correctly and receive the information they need, and preserves knowledge for the team.

## 2. Scope

These procedures apply to all regular full-time and part-time employees joining or leaving Lumora, in the Austin and Raleigh offices and in remote locations across the United States. Interns and temporary employees follow the same steps unless their program coordinator says otherwise. Contractors follow a shortened version managed by the hiring manager and IT, but the access and equipment rules in sections 9 and 10 apply to them in full.

Internal transfers and promotions are not covered here; see HR-005.

## 3. Definitions

- Hire date or start date: The first day the employee is scheduled to work and be paid.
- Pre-boarding: The period between offer acceptance and the start date.
- Introductory period: The first 90 days of employment, during which the employee and manager confirm that the role is a good fit.
- Onboarding buddy: An experienced employee, outside the new hire's reporting line, assigned to help the new hire settle in.
- Voluntary separation: An employee's decision to leave Lumora, such as a resignation or retirement.
- Involuntary separation: A separation decided by Lumora, such as a termination for performance or conduct, or a position elimination.
- Last day: The employee's final working day at Lumora.
- Knowledge transfer plan: A written plan that lists a departing employee's responsibilities, open work, key contacts and documentation, and who will take each item over.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

The hiring or departing manager is responsible for:

- Planning the new hire's first weeks, assigning the onboarding buddy, and holding the 30/60/90-day check-ins.
- Entering resignations and other departures in Workday on time.
- Approving and overseeing the knowledge transfer plan.

HR (People team) is responsible for:

- Sending offer letters, starting the background check, and managing Form I-9 compliance.
- Running new hire orientation and assigning mandatory training in Workday.
- Processing separations, final pay, COBRA information, and the exit interview.

IT is responsible for:

- Preparing and shipping laptops, creating accounts, and recovering equipment.
- Disabling access on time, as required by SEC-001.

Security is responsible for overseeing access revocation for involuntary terminations and for reviewing any data handling concerns during offboarding.

## 5. Pre-boarding: From Offer to First Day

### 5.1 Offer acceptance

Offers are sent and signed electronically. Once a candidate accepts the offer, the recruiter marks the offer as accepted in Workday, which creates the pre-hire record and starts the pre-boarding checklist. The offer letter states the start date, base salary, bonus target under HR-005, work arrangement under HR-004 (On-site, Hybrid or Remote), and work location.

### 5.2 Background check

After offer acceptance, HR starts a background check through Lumora's screening vendor. The background check normally takes 5 to 7 business days. The candidate receives an email from the vendor and must complete the consent form promptly, because the clock does not start until consent is given. Background checks are carried out in line with the Fair Credit Reporting Act and applicable state and local laws. If the report contains information that may affect the offer, HR follows the required pre-adverse action process, including giving the candidate a copy of the report and a chance to respond.

Start dates are confirmed only after the background check is cleared. To allow time for the check and for equipment shipping, HR recommends a start date at least 2 weeks after offer acceptance.

### 5.3 Equipment shipping

IT ships a configured laptop and accessories to the new hire's home address, or holds it in the office for on-site employees who prefer to collect it. Laptops are shipped about 5 business days before the start date so that they arrive at least 2 business days before the start date. The laptop is already encrypted and enrolled in device management. New hires should not try to log in before their first day; they will receive login instructions at orientation.

If the laptop has not arrived 2 business days before the start date, the new hire should email it-help@lumora.example or have the hiring manager open a ServiceNow ticket in the Lumora Help portal.

### 5.4 Pre-boarding tasks in Workday

Before the first day, the new hire completes these tasks in the Workday pre-hire portal:

- Personal information and emergency contacts.
- Direct deposit details for payroll.
- Federal and state tax withholding forms.
- Form I-9 Section 1, which may be completed any time after offer acceptance and must be completed no later than the first day of employment.
- Acknowledgment of receipt of the Employee Handbook (HR-001).

### 5.5 Manager preparation

Before the start date, the hiring manager:

- Assigns an onboarding buddy and introduces them by email.
- Requests role-specific system access through ServiceNow, following least privilege under SEC-001.
- Prepares a first-week schedule, including a welcome meeting, team introductions and the first 1:1.
- Adds the new hire to team Slack channels and recurring meetings.

## 6. First Day and First Week

### 6.1 New hire orientation

Orientation is held on the first day and is run by HR, with sessions from IT and Security. It covers Lumora's history and products, values and the Code of Conduct, benefits enrollment, pay and Workday basics, and IT setup. Remote employees attend orientation by video.

During orientation, IT helps the new hire sign in to the laptop, set up 1Password, and enroll in multi-factor authentication with Okta Verify or a hardware key, as required by SEC-001.

### 6.2 Form I-9 verification

Federal law requires every new employee to complete Form I-9 to verify identity and work authorization. The employee completes Section 1 by the first day. The employee must then present original, acceptable identity and work authorization documents, and HR must verify them and complete Section 2 by the 3rd business day after the start date. For example, for an employee who starts on a Monday, the documents must be verified by Thursday of that week. Remote employees present their documents to an authorized representative or through the remote verification method HR provides. An employee who cannot provide acceptable documents by the 3rd business day may not continue working until they do, except where a receipt for a replacement document is allowed by law.

### 6.3 Benefits enrollment

New employees must enroll in benefits in Workday within 30 days of hire. Coverage is effective from the first day of employment. Details are in HR-006.

### 6.4 First week with the manager

During the first week, the manager meets the new hire to review the role, the team's goals, how success will be measured in the first 90 days, and the expectations for working hours, including core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 15:00 local time. The manager also sets up a recurring 1:1, at least every 2 weeks, as required by HR-005.

## 7. Mandatory Training

All new employees must complete the following trainings within their first 30 days. Trainings are assigned automatically in Workday and tracked to completion.

- Security awareness training, as required by SEC-001.
- Anti-harassment training, as required by HR-007.
- Privacy training, as required by LEG-001.
- Code of conduct training, including the Code of Conduct acknowledgment under HR-001.

Managers receive reminders when trainings are overdue. Access to some systems that hold Confidential or Restricted data may be withheld until security and privacy training are complete. Role-specific training, such as secure coding for engineers or sales compliance for account executives, is assigned by the manager in addition to these four.

## 8. The First 90 Days

### 8.1 Introductory period

The first 90 days of employment are the introductory period. During this time the manager and the new hire assess whether the role is a good fit, and the manager gives frequent, direct feedback. Completing the introductory period does not change Lumora's at-will employment relationship and does not guarantee employment for any period. Benefits eligibility and PTO accrual are not affected by the introductory period; new hires accrue PTO from day one under HR-002.

### 8.2 30/60/90-day check-ins

The manager holds structured check-ins with the new hire at 30, 60 and 90 days, and records each one in Workday:

- 30-day check-in: confirm that equipment, access and mandatory training are complete; review early impressions; agree on goals for the next 60 days.
- 60-day check-in: review progress against goals; give and receive feedback; identify any gaps in skills or support.
- 90-day check-in: confirm successful completion of the introductory period, set goals for the rest of the year, and agree on development priorities.

If there are performance concerns, the manager raises them as early as possible, and should involve the HR Business Partner before the 90-day check-in. The introductory period may be extended once, with HR approval, if more time is needed to assess fit.

### 8.3 Onboarding buddy

Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy. The buddy meets the new hire in the first week and then at least weekly for the first month, answers practical questions, explains team norms, and introduces the new hire to people across the company. The buddy is not responsible for evaluating the new hire's performance.

### 8.4 Home-office setup

Hybrid and Remote employees may claim the one-time home-office stipend within their first 90 days, and the monthly internet reimbursement, under HR-004.

## 9. Offboarding: Notice and Planning

### 9.1 Notice period for resignations

Lumora asks employees who resign to give at least 2 weeks' notice. Employees at the manager level and above are asked to give at least 4 weeks' notice. Notice should be given in writing to the manager, by email or through Workday.

### 9.2 Manager notification to HR

When an employee resigns, the manager must enter the resignation in Workday and notify HR at least 2 weeks before the employee's last day. If the employee gives less notice than that, the manager enters the resignation the same business day it is received. Entering the separation in Workday triggers the offboarding checklist for HR, IT, Payroll and Security.

### 9.3 Involuntary separations

For involuntary separations, the manager must plan with the HR Business Partner in advance. HR prepares the separation documents, coordinates the timing with IT and Security, and attends the separation meeting. Managers must not tell an employee about an involuntary separation before HR has approved it.

### 9.4 Knowledge transfer plan

A knowledge transfer plan is required for every departing employee. The departing employee drafts the plan with the manager within the first few days after notice is given. The plan should list:

- Current projects and their status, with links to the documentation.
- Recurring responsibilities, such as reports, on-call rotations and customer meetings.
- Key internal and external contacts.
- Systems or accounts the employee owns or administers, including shared credentials stored in 1Password team vaults.
- The person who will take over each item.

The manager approves the plan and confirms it is complete before the last day. For involuntary separations, the manager prepares the plan without the employee where necessary.

### 9.5 Handling company data

Departing employees must not copy, forward or download company data to personal devices, personal email or personal cloud storage. Auto-forwarding of company email to personal accounts is prohibited under SEC-002. Company files must stay in company systems. Security may review unusual data downloads during the notice period.

## 10. Offboarding: Last Day and After

### 10.1 Access revocation

For voluntary separations, access to all company systems is revoked at the end of the employee's last working day. For involuntary separations, access is revoked immediately, at the time the employee is notified. IT and Security confirm that all accounts, including Okta, Slack, email, VPN and any customer-facing systems, have been disabled. SEC-001 requires that access is removed within 24 hours of termination in all cases.

### 10.2 Returning equipment

Departing employees must return all company equipment, including the laptop, charger, monitors and other peripherals, security keys and badges, within 10 business days after their last day. IT sends a prepaid return box to remote employees. Office-based employees may return equipment to IT in person on their last day. Employees must not wipe or reset the laptop; IT does this after the equipment is received.

If equipment is not returned within 10 business days, IT and HR follow up. Lumora may recover the cost of unreturned equipment where state law permits.

### 10.3 Expenses and corporate card

Departing employees should submit any outstanding expense reports in SAP Concur before their last day, following FIN-001. Corporate cards are cancelled on the last day, and any personal charges must be repaid.

### 10.4 Final pay

The final paycheck is issued according to state law. Depending on the state and the type of separation, it may be issued on the last day, within a set number of days, or on the next regular pay date. Lumora's regular pay dates are the 15th and the last business day of each month. The final paycheck includes wages through the last day and payout of accrued unused PTO under HR-002. Sick leave is not paid out. Eligibility for the prior year's annual bonus depends on being employed on the payout date, as described in HR-005.

### 10.5 Benefits and COBRA

Medical, dental and vision coverage end on the last day of the month in which employment ends. COBRA continuation information is sent to the employee within 14 days of separation. Information about converting life insurance and managing the 401(k) and HSA is included in the separation packet and in HR-006.

### 10.6 Exit interview

An exit interview is offered to every employee who leaves voluntarily. It is conducted by HR, not the manager, either by video or through a confidential Workday survey. Participation is optional. HR shares themes from exit interviews with leaders in aggregate, without identifying individuals, unless the employee raises a concern that must be investigated under HR-007.

### 10.7 Records

Personnel records for former employees are kept for 7 years after separation, as required by LEG-001. Former employees may request employment verification through hr@lumora.example.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

### When will my laptop arrive?

IT ships it about 5 business days before your start date so that it arrives at least 2 business days before you start. If it has not arrived by then, contact it-help@lumora.example.

### How long does the background check take?

Usually 5 to 7 business days after you give consent to the screening vendor.

### What documents do I need for Form I-9?

You complete Section 1 by your first day, and you must show original identity and work authorization documents so HR can verify them by your 3rd business day.

### Which trainings must I finish in my first month?

Security awareness, anti-harassment, privacy, and code of conduct training, all within your first 30 days.

### How long is the introductory period?

90 days, with check-ins with your manager at 30, 60 and 90 days.

### How much notice should I give when I resign?

At least 2 weeks. If you are a manager or above, at least 4 weeks.

### How long do I have to return my laptop after I leave?

10 business days after your last day. IT will send you a prepaid return box if you work remotely.

### When will I lose access to my accounts?

At the end of your last working day for a resignation. For involuntary terminations, access is removed immediately.
