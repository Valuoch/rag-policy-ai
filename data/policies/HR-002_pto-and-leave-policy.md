## 1. Purpose

Lumora Systems, Inc. ("Lumora") wants employees to rest, recover, care for their families, and take part in their communities. This policy explains the types of paid and unpaid time off available, how much time employees receive, how to request it in Workday, and what happens to unused time at year end and at separation. It is designed to be applied consistently across teams so that every employee knows what to expect.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time Lumora employees, whether they work On-site, Hybrid, or Remote. Part-time employees accrue PTO and receive sick leave on a prorated basis in proportion to their scheduled hours, unless state or local law requires more. Temporary employees and interns receive sick leave as required by applicable law and are otherwise not eligible for PTO unless their offer letter states otherwise. Contractors are not covered.

Where a state or local law (for example, a paid sick leave or family leave law) provides more generous rights than this policy, Lumora follows the law. Company holidays and floating holidays are covered in HR-003 2026 Holiday Calendar & Holiday Policy.

## 3. Definitions

- **PTO (Paid Time Off)**: Paid time used for vacation, personal business, or other personal reasons. PTO is separate from sick leave.
- **Years of service**: Completed years of continuous employment with Lumora, measured from the employee's hire date as recorded in Workday.
- **Pay period**: One of the 24 semi-monthly pay periods per year. Payroll is paid on the 15th and the last business day of each month.
- **Accrual**: The gradual earning of PTO each pay period.
- **Carryover**: Unused PTO moved from one calendar year to the next.
- **Immediate family**: Spouse or domestic partner, child (including step, foster, and adopted), parent (including step and in-law), sibling, grandparent, grandchild, and any person living in the employee's household.
- **Extended family**: Aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, cousin, and sibling-in-law.
- **Birthing / primary caregiver**: The parent who gives birth, or the parent with primary responsibility for the care of a newly born, adopted, or foster-placed child.
- **Secondary caregiver**: A parent who is not the birthing or primary caregiver.
- **Business day**: Monday through Friday, excluding company holidays.

## 4. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### 4.1 PTO accrual by tenure

PTO accrual depends on years of service:

| Years of service | Annual PTO | Accrual per pay period (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 years | 15 days per year | 0.625 days |
| 3-5 years | 20 days per year | 0.833 days |
| 6+ years | 25 days per year | 1.042 days |

Employees with 0-2 years of service accrue 15 days of PTO per year. Employees with 3-5 years of service accrue 20 days of PTO per year. Employees with 6 or more years of service accrue 25 days of PTO per year.

PTO accrues each pay period at 1/24 of the annual amount. The higher accrual rate begins with the first pay period after the employee's service anniversary (for example, the third anniversary moves an employee from 15 to 20 days per year). Workday updates the rate automatically.

### 4.2 New hires

New hires accrue PTO from their first day of employment; there is no waiting period. Because a new hire has a small balance at first, an employee may go up to 5 days negative PTO balance with manager approval. A negative balance is repaid automatically through future accruals. If an employee leaves with a negative balance, the value may be recovered from final pay where state law allows.

### 4.3 Using PTO

PTO may be taken in full-day or half-day increments. PTO is recorded in hours in Workday (one day equals 8 hours for a full-time employee). Employees should coordinate with their team so that customer commitments and on-call coverage are maintained.

A company holiday that falls during approved PTO is not charged as PTO. For example, if you take the week of Thanksgiving off, Thanksgiving Day and the day after Thanksgiving are recorded as holidays, not PTO.

### 4.4 Carryover and year end

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year or they are forfeited. Any unused PTO above 5 days at December 31 is forfeited, except where state law prohibits forfeiture. Workday applies carried-over days first when PTO is taken between January 1 and March 31.

**Example:** An employee has 8 unused days on December 31, 2026. Five days carry over into 2027 and must be used by March 31, 2027. The remaining 3 days are forfeited.

### 4.5 PTO payout at separation

When an employee leaves Lumora, accrued unused PTO is paid out. Where state law requires payout of all accrued PTO, all of it is paid. Otherwise, accrued unused PTO is paid out up to a maximum of 10 days. Payout is included in the final paycheck, which is issued in accordance with state law. Sick leave is never paid out (see Section 6). Offboarding steps are described in HR-008.

## 5. Requesting PTO

### 5.1 Notice requirements

All PTO requests are submitted in Workday.

- For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, submit the request at least 2 weeks in advance.
- For shorter absences (1-2 days or half days), submit the request at least 48 hours in advance.

Managers may approve requests with less notice at their discretion. Emergencies are handled under Section 5.4.

### 5.2 How to request PTO in Workday

1. Sign in to Workday and select **Absence**, then **Request Absence**.
2. Choose the dates and select **PTO** as the absence type (use **Floating Holiday** for floating holidays).
3. Enter the hours per day if you are taking a half day.
4. Add an optional comment (for example, coverage arrangements).
5. Submit. Your manager receives a Workday notification.
6. Add the time to your shared calendar and set an out-of-office message in email and a status in Slack.

### 5.3 Manager approval

Managers approve or deny PTO requests within 3 business days. Requests are normally approved unless the absence would create an unacceptable gap in coverage, overlaps a critical business period (for example, a major customer launch), or conflicts with a request already approved for the same team. When several people request the same dates, managers should generally approve requests in the order received and rotate popular periods fairly from year to year. A denial must include a reason and, where possible, alternative dates.

### 5.4 Unplanned absences

If you need unplanned time off, notify your manager as early as possible, by Slack or phone, and before the start of your workday where possible. Enter the absence in Workday when you return, choosing PTO or sick leave as appropriate.

## 6. Sick Leave

### 6.1 Amount and timing

Sick leave is a separate bank of 10 days per year. It is front-loaded on January 1 of each year, so the full amount is available immediately. New hires receive a prorated amount on their start date based on the portion of the calendar year remaining (for example, an employee who starts on July 1 receives 5 days).

Sick leave does not carry over to the next year and is not paid out at separation. Where a state or city sick leave law requires carryover or a different accrual method, Lumora complies with that law for employees in that location.

### 6.2 Permitted uses

Sick leave may be used for:

- The employee's own illness, injury, medical appointment, or preventive care.
- Caring for an immediate family member who is ill, injured, or has a medical appointment.
- Absences related to domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking, where state law provides.
- Mental health days.

### 6.3 Medical documentation

A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days. The note should confirm that the absence was medically necessary and, if relevant, the expected return date or any work restrictions. It should not include a diagnosis. Notes are submitted to HR at hr@lumora.example, not to the manager, and are stored confidentially.

If sick leave is exhausted, an employee may use PTO. Absences longer than a few weeks may qualify for short-term disability (see HR-006 Employee Benefits Guide) or FMLA leave (Section 8.2).

## 7. Other Paid Leave

### 7.1 Bereavement leave

Employees receive 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member and 2 paid days for the death of an extended family member. The days need not be consecutive but should be used within a reasonable time of the death, for example to attend a funeral or settle affairs. Employees may use PTO for additional time. Request bereavement leave in Workday; no documentation is normally required.

### 7.2 Jury duty and witness service

Employees summoned for jury duty receive full pay for up to 10 working days per year. Provide a copy of the summons to your manager and submit the absence as **Jury Duty** in Workday. If jury service continues beyond 10 working days, the employee may use PTO or take unpaid, job-protected leave as required by law. Employees released early on a given day are expected to work the remainder of the day if practical.

### 7.3 Parental leave

Lumora provides fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child:

- **Birthing / primary caregiver**: 16 weeks fully paid.
- **Secondary caregiver**: 8 weeks fully paid.

Eligibility and rules:

- Employees are eligible after 6 months of employment.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption (or foster placement).
- Leave can be taken in up to 2 blocks (for example, 10 weeks after birth and 6 weeks later in the year).
- Parental leave runs concurrently with FMLA leave and any state family leave program where the employee is eligible. Short-term disability benefits for a birthing parent are coordinated so that total pay does not exceed 100% of base pay.
- Notify your manager and HR at least 30 days before the expected start of leave when the date is foreseeable.

PTO and sick leave continue to accrue during parental leave, and benefits continue on the same terms as for active employees.

### 7.4 Volunteer time off (VTO)

Employees receive 1 paid volunteer day per year to volunteer with a registered non-profit organization, school, or community group. VTO does not carry over and is not paid out. Request it in Workday as **Volunteer Time Off**.

### 7.5 Sabbatical

Employees become eligible for a 4-week paid sabbatical after 7 years of continuous service. The sabbatical must be scheduled with at least 3 months' notice, agreed with the manager, and entered in Workday. A sabbatical is taken as a single continuous block and does not reduce the employee's PTO balance. Eligibility renews after each further 7 years of continuous service.

## 8. Unpaid and Job-Protected Leave

### 8.1 Leave of absence

Employees who need extended time away that is not covered by another leave type may request an unpaid personal leave of absence. Requests are reviewed by HR and the employee's manager case by case, considering business needs and the reason for the request.

### 8.2 Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)

Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave in a 12-month period under the federal Family and Medical Leave Act. To be eligible, an employee must have worked for Lumora for 12 months and have worked at least 1,250 hours in the 12 months before the leave begins.

Qualifying reasons include the employee's own serious health condition, caring for a spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition, the birth or placement of a child, and certain military family needs. Group health coverage continues during FMLA leave. Paid leave types such as parental leave, sick leave, and short-term disability run concurrently with FMLA where they apply.

To request FMLA leave, contact HR at hr@lumora.example at least 30 days in advance when the need is foreseeable, or as soon as practicable otherwise. HR will provide the required notices and certification forms.

### 8.3 Military leave

Employees called to military service receive job-protected leave as required by the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA) and applicable state law.

### 8.4 Disability and accommodation

Employees who cannot work because of a medical condition may be eligible for short-term or long-term disability benefits (see HR-006). Employees who need a workplace accommodation should contact HR; leave may itself be a reasonable accommodation.

## 9. Manager Responsibilities

Managers must:

- Respond to PTO requests in Workday within 3 business days.
- Apply this policy consistently and fairly across their team.
- Encourage employees to take their PTO during the year, and especially to use carried-over days before March 31.
- Never ask employees to work while on approved leave, except in a genuine emergency that the employee agrees to help with.
- Keep medical information confidential and refer leave questions involving health to HR.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. I have been at Lumora for 4 years. How much PTO do I get?**
Employees with 3-5 years of service accrue 20 days of PTO per year, accruing about 0.833 days each pay period.

**Q2. How many PTO days can I carry over into next year?**
A maximum of 5 unused days. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they are forfeited.

**Q3. How far in advance do I need to request a week of vacation?**
For 3 or more consecutive days, request at least 2 weeks in advance in Workday. Your manager will respond within 3 business days.

**Q4. Is my sick leave paid out when I leave?**
No. Sick leave (10 days per year, front-loaded January 1) is not paid out and does not carry over. Accrued unused PTO is paid out: all of it where state law requires, otherwise up to 10 days.

**Q5. When do I need a doctor's note?**
After 3 consecutive sick days.

**Q6. I joined Lumora 4 months ago and am expecting a baby. Am I eligible for parental leave?**
Parental leave eligibility begins after 6 months of employment. If your child is born before you reach 6 months, contact HR; you can begin your parental leave once you become eligible, provided it is taken within 12 months of the birth.

**Q7. Can I split my parental leave?**
Yes. Parental leave can be taken in up to 2 blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

**Q8. Does a holiday during my vacation count against my PTO?**
No. A company holiday that falls during approved PTO is not charged as PTO.
