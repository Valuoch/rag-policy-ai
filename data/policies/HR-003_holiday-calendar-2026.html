1. Purpose

This policy publishes the paid company holidays that Lumora Systems, Inc. ("Lumora") observes in calendar year 2026 and explains the rules that apply to them: how holidays are observed when they fall on a weekend, how floating holidays work, how holidays interact with paid time off and other leave, and how employees who must work on a holiday are paid and compensated. Publishing the calendar at the start of the year helps employees plan time off and helps managers plan coverage for customers.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all Lumora employees in the United States, regardless of work arrangement (On-site, Hybrid, or Remote). Full-time employees receive holiday pay as described below. Part-time employees receive holiday pay prorated in proportion to their regularly scheduled hours. Temporary employees and interns receive holiday pay only if their offer letter says so. Contractors, consultants, and vendor staff are not covered by this policy; their holiday arrangements are governed by their contracts.

Paid time off, sick leave, parental leave, and other leave types are governed by HR-002 Paid Time Off & Leave Policy. Where this policy refers to requesting time off in Workday, the notice requirements in HR-002 apply.

3. Definitions

  • Company holiday: A day on which Lumora offices are closed and employees receive their regular pay without working, as listed in Section 4.
  • Observed date: The weekday on which Lumora observes a holiday whose actual date falls on a Saturday or Sunday.
  • Floating holiday: A paid day off that the employee chooses, used at any time in the calendar year, as described in Section 6.
  • Exempt employee: A salaried employee who is not eligible for overtime under the Fair Labor Standards Act.
  • Non-exempt employee: An employee who is eligible for overtime and records hours worked in Workday.
  • Substitute day off: A paid day off given in place of a holiday that an employee was required to work.
  • Holiday coverage: Staffing arranged in advance so that critical functions, such as customer support and production operations, continue during a holiday.

4.2 Planning notes for 2026

  • Long weekends. Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, and Labor Day each create a three-day weekend. Juneteenth (Friday, June 19) and Independence Day observed (Friday, July 3) create three-day weekends two weeks apart.
  • Thanksgiving. Thanksgiving Day and the day after Thanksgiving create a four-day weekend from Thursday, November 26 through Sunday, November 29.
  • Year-end. Christmas Eve and Christmas Day fall on Thursday and Friday, creating a four-day weekend from Thursday, December 24 through Sunday, December 27. Employees who want to take the remaining days of that week (December 28 through December 31) off should request PTO or use a floating holiday in Workday as early as possible, because many requests are made for this period.
  • New Year's Day 2027. New Year's Day 2027 falls on a Friday and will be listed in the 2027 calendar; it is not a 2026 holiday.
  • Payroll. None of the 2026 company holidays falls on a scheduled payday. The last business day of December 2026 is Thursday, December 31, which is the final payday of the year.

5. Observance Rules

5.1 Weekend holidays

When a company holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When a company holiday falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday. In 2026, this rule applies to Independence Day: July 4 is a Saturday, so Lumora observes the holiday on Friday, July 3.

5.2 Local time and location

Holidays are observed on the listed date in the employee's local time zone. All US employees observe the same holiday list, whether they are based in Austin, in Raleigh, or remotely in another state. Lumora does not substitute state-specific holidays for company holidays; employees who wish to observe a state, cultural, or religious holiday that is not on the list may use a floating holiday or PTO (see Section 6 and Section 8).

5.3 Office closures

The Austin and Raleigh offices are closed on company holidays. Badge access may be limited, and building services such as reception and catering are not available. Employees who need to enter an office on a holiday for an approved business reason should arrange access in advance with Workplace Services through the Lumora Help portal in ServiceNow.

6. Floating Holidays

6.1 Entitlement

Each eligible employee receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year. Floating holidays allow employees to observe days that matter to them personally, such as religious or cultural holidays, a birthday, or a family event, without using PTO.

6.2 Rules for using floating holidays

  • Floating holidays may be used at any time during the calendar year.
  • Floating holidays must be used in the calendar year in which they are granted. There is no carryover; unused floating holidays expire on December 31.
  • Floating holidays are not paid out at separation.
  • Each floating holiday is taken as a full day.
  • Floating holidays are requested like PTO: submit the request in Workday, selecting Floating Holiday as the absence type, and follow the notice requirements in HR-002 (at least 48 hours' notice for a single day, or at least 2 weeks' notice if it is part of an absence of 3 or more consecutive days). The manager approves or denies within 3 business days.

6.3 Floating holidays for new hires

New hires receive floating holidays prorated by start date:

  • Employees who start before July 1 receive 2 floating holidays for that calendar year.
  • Employees who start on or after July 1 receive 1 floating holiday for that calendar year.

For example, an employee who starts on June 29, 2026 receives 2 floating holidays for 2026, while an employee who starts on July 1, 2026 receives 1 floating holiday for 2026. Floating holidays are available in Workday from the employee's first day.

6.4 How to request a floating holiday

  1. Sign in to Workday and select Absence, then Request Absence.
  2. Select the date and choose Floating Holiday as the absence type.
  3. Optionally add a comment, such as the occasion or your coverage plan. You are not required to state the reason.
  4. Submit the request. Your manager is notified and responds within 3 business days.
  5. Once approved, add the day to your calendar and set your out-of-office and Slack status.

7. Holidays and Other Time Off

7.1 Holidays during approved PTO

A company holiday that falls during approved PTO is not charged as PTO. Workday records the day as a holiday automatically. For example, an employee who takes PTO for the full week of November 23 through November 27, 2026 uses 3 days of PTO (Monday through Wednesday); Thanksgiving Day and the day after Thanksgiving are recorded as holidays.

7.2 Holidays during sick leave

A company holiday that falls during a period of sick leave is recorded as a holiday and is not deducted from the employee's sick leave bank.

7.3 Holidays during other leaves

Parental leave and sabbaticals are measured in calendar weeks, so a holiday that falls within the leave does not extend it. Employees on unpaid leaves of absence, including unpaid FMLA leave, are not paid for holidays that fall during the unpaid period, except where required by law. Employees receiving disability benefits are paid according to the disability plan described in HR-006 Employee Benefits Guide rather than receiving separate holiday pay.

7.4 Holidays and the introductory period

New employees are eligible for company holidays from their first day of employment. There is no waiting period, including during the 90-day introductory period described in HR-008 Onboarding & Offboarding Procedures.

8. Religious and Cultural Observances

Lumora respects the diverse religious and cultural traditions of its employees. Employees who wish to observe a religious or cultural holiday that is not a company holiday may use a floating holiday, PTO, or, with manager agreement, a flexible schedule that makes up the time within the same workweek, provided core collaboration hours (10:00 to 15:00 local time) are covered on other days. Managers should make reasonable efforts to avoid scheduling mandatory meetings, launches, or deadlines on widely observed religious holidays, and should grant reasonable requests for religious accommodation in line with HR-007 Anti-Harassment, Equal Opportunity & Workplace Conduct Policy.

9. Working on a Holiday

9.1 When work may be required

Most employees do not work on company holidays. Some roles, such as customer support, site reliability and production operations, and security operations, may need to provide coverage on a holiday to meet customer commitments. Managers who need holiday coverage must publish a holiday coverage schedule at least 30 days before the holiday where practical, ask for volunteers first, and rotate holiday assignments fairly across the team over the year.

9.2 Non-exempt employees

Non-exempt employees who are required to work on a company holiday receive 1.5 times their regular rate of pay for all hours worked on the holiday, plus a substitute day off. The substitute day off must be taken within 60 days of the holiday worked and is requested in Workday as Holiday Substitute. All hours worked on a holiday must be recorded in Workday.

Example: A non-exempt support specialist works 8 hours on Monday, September 7, 2026 (Labor Day). The employee is paid for those 8 hours at 1.5 times the regular rate and takes a substitute day off on a date agreed with the manager no later than Friday, November 6, 2026.

9.3 Exempt employees

Exempt employees who are asked by their manager to work on a company holiday should agree a substitute day off with their manager, recorded in Workday. Exempt employees who choose to do a small amount of work on a holiday for their own convenience are not entitled to a substitute day.

9.4 On-call rotations

Employees in an on-call rotation that includes a holiday should plan with their team in advance. On-call arrangements and any associated pay are set out by the relevant department and must comply with this policy for non-exempt employees.

10. Holidays and Business Travel

Employees should avoid scheduling business travel on company holidays unless it is necessary for a customer commitment. If travel on a holiday is required, it should be approved in advance by the manager as part of pre-trip approval under FIN-002 Business Travel Policy. Non-exempt employees who travel for business on a holiday are treated as working on the holiday for the hours that count as work time under applicable law.

11. Responsibilities

  • Employees plan time off around the published calendar, request floating holidays and PTO in Workday with the required notice, and record any holiday hours worked.
  • Managers plan holiday coverage, approve or deny requests within 3 business days, ensure substitute days off are taken within 60 days, and treat holiday assignments fairly.
  • HR publishes the holiday calendar each year, configures Workday, answers questions at hr@lumora.example, and reviews this policy annually.
  • Payroll processes holiday pay and the 1.5 times premium for non-exempt employees in the semi-monthly payroll.

12. Frequently Asked Questions

How many paid holidays does Lumora have in 2026?

Lumora has 11 paid company holidays in 2026, plus 2 floating holidays for each full-year employee.

Why is Independence Day on July 3 in 2026?

July 4, 2026 falls on a Saturday. Holidays that fall on a Saturday are observed on the preceding Friday, so the holiday is observed on Friday, July 3, 2026. A holiday that falls on a Sunday is observed on the following Monday.

Can I carry an unused floating holiday into 2027?

No. Floating holidays must be used within the calendar year; there is no carryover.

I start on August 3, 2026. How many floating holidays do I get?

One. New hires who start on or after July 1 receive 1 floating holiday for that year; those who start before July 1 receive 2.

If I take the week of Christmas off, how much PTO do I use?

Christmas Eve (Thursday, December 24) and Christmas Day (Friday, December 25) are company holidays and are not charged as PTO. Taking Monday, December 21 through Friday, December 25 off uses 3 days of PTO.

I am a non-exempt employee and was scheduled to work on Thanksgiving. How am I compensated?

You are paid 1.5 times your regular rate for the hours worked on Thanksgiving and receive a substitute day off, which must be taken within 60 days.

Do I need to give a reason for using a floating holiday?

No. You request floating holidays in Workday like PTO; the reason is optional.

Is the day after Thanksgiving a company holiday?

Yes. The day after Thanksgiving, Friday, November 27, 2026, is one of the 11 paid company holidays.
