## 1. Purpose

This Employee Handbook and Code of Conduct describes how Lumora Systems, Inc. ("Lumora" or "the company") expects its people to work together, treat customers and colleagues, and protect the company's reputation and assets. It is the starting point for every employee: it explains the company's basic employment practices, sets the standards of ethical behavior that apply to everyone, and points to the more detailed policies that govern specific topics such as leave, benefits, security, and travel.

Lumora is a B2B analytics SaaS company founded in 2014. We have approximately 850 employees, a headquarters in Austin, Texas, a second office in Raleigh, North Carolina, and remote colleagues across many US states. As we have grown, we have kept one principle constant: our customers trust us with their data and their decisions, and that trust depends on each of us acting with integrity every day.

This handbook is not a contract of employment and does not change the at-will nature of employment where at-will employment applies. Where a state or local law grants employees greater rights than this handbook, Lumora follows the law.

## 2. Scope

This handbook applies to:

- All Lumora employees, regardless of level, location, or work arrangement.
- Managers, directors, vice presidents, and executive officers, who are held to an even higher standard because they model behavior for their teams.
- Temporary employees and interns employed directly by Lumora.

Contractors, consultants, and vendor staff are not Lumora employees, but they are expected to follow the Code of Conduct sections (Sections 6 through 12) while working on Lumora business or on Lumora premises. Their sponsoring manager is responsible for making them aware of these expectations.

## 3. Definitions

- **Code of Conduct**: Sections 6 through 12 of this document, which set out the ethical standards every employee must follow.
- **Manager**: The person to whom an employee directly reports, as recorded in Workday.
- **Indirect report**: Any employee who reports to a manager's direct reports, at any level below them in the reporting chain.
- **Conflict of interest**: Any situation in which an employee's personal interests, relationships, or activities could interfere, or appear to interfere, with their ability to act in the best interests of Lumora.
- **Nominal value**: A gift or item of hospitality with a modest market value, subject to the $100 per year per source limit described in Section 8.
- **Cash equivalent**: Gift cards, prepaid cards, vouchers, securities, cryptocurrency, or any item that can readily be converted to cash.
- **Ethics & Compliance hotline**: Lumora's anonymous, 24/7 reporting line, reachable at 1-800-555-0142.
- **Retaliation**: Any adverse action taken against a person because they raised a concern in good faith or participated in an investigation.

## 4. Employment Basics

### 4.1 Work arrangements

Lumora supports three work arrangements: On-site, Hybrid, and Remote. Hybrid is the default arrangement for office-assigned roles. Attendance expectations, home-office stipends, and rules for working from another state or country are set out in HR-004 Remote & Hybrid Work Policy.

### 4.2 Workweek and core hours

The standard workweek at Lumora is 40 hours. All employees, wherever they work, are expected to be available during core collaboration hours, which are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule with their manager, provided team needs and customer commitments are met.

Non-exempt (hourly) employees must accurately record all time worked in Workday, including any time worked outside normal hours. Working "off the clock" is not permitted, and managers may not ask employees to do so.

### 4.3 Payroll

Lumora pays employees semi-monthly, on the 15th and on the last business day of each month, for a total of 24 pay periods per year. If the 15th falls on a weekend or a bank holiday, pay is issued on the preceding business day. Employees can view pay statements, tax forms, and direct deposit settings in Workday. Questions about pay should be directed to hr@lumora.example.

### 4.4 Introductory period

All new employees complete a 90-day introductory period. During this period the employee and manager hold 30-, 60-, and 90-day check-ins to review progress. The introductory period does not change the at-will nature of employment, and completing it does not create a guarantee of continued employment. Onboarding steps are described in HR-008 Onboarding & Offboarding Procedures.

### 4.5 Personnel records

Workday is Lumora's system of record for employee information. Employees are responsible for keeping their home address, emergency contacts, tax withholding, and bank details current. A change of home state must be reported to HR before the move, because it can affect payroll tax registration.

### 4.6 Tools you will use

- **Workday**: HR records, time off requests, pay statements, performance reviews.
- **SAP Concur**: Expense reports and reimbursements (see FIN-001).
- **Navan**: Business travel booking (see FIN-002).
- **ServiceNow ("Lumora Help" portal)**: IT support tickets and access requests.
- **Slack**: Day-to-day chat and team communication.

## 5. Equal Opportunity and Respectful Workplace

Lumora is an equal opportunity employer. Employment decisions are based on merit, qualifications, and business needs, and never on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, pregnancy, or any other characteristic protected by law.

Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are not tolerated. The full standards, examples, and complaint procedure are set out in HR-007 Anti-Harassment, Equal Opportunity & Workplace Conduct Policy. This handbook summarizes the reporting and non-retaliation commitments in Section 12 so that every employee knows how to raise a concern.

## 6. Code of Conduct: Core Standards

Every Lumora employee is expected to:

1. **Act with integrity.** Be honest in your work, your records, and your communications. Never falsify timesheets, expense reports, sales forecasts, or customer data.
2. **Protect customer trust.** Handle customer data only as needed for your job and in line with SEC-001 Information Security Policy and LEG-001 Data Privacy & Protection Policy.
3. **Treat people with respect.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Be professional in meetings, in Slack, and in email.
4. **Follow the law.** Comply with all laws and regulations that apply to our business, including anti-bribery, antitrust, export control, and privacy laws.
5. **Speak up.** If you see something that seems wrong, raise it. You will not be penalized for reporting a concern in good faith.

### 6.1 Annual Code of Conduct acknowledgment

All employees must acknowledge the Code of Conduct annually in Workday. New hires complete the code of conduct training and acknowledgment as one of the mandatory trainings within their first 30 days of employment. Managers receive a report of team members who have not completed the acknowledgment and are expected to follow up.

### 6.2 Accurate records

Business records, including financial records, contracts, customer communications, and performance documentation, must be complete and accurate. Records must be retained according to the schedules in LEG-001. Never destroy or alter records that are subject to a legal hold, audit, or investigation.

## 7. Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest exists whenever a personal interest could influence, or appear to influence, your judgment on behalf of Lumora. Having a potential conflict is not itself a violation; failing to disclose it is.

### 7.1 Common examples

- Owning a significant financial interest in a competitor, customer, or supplier.
- Holding a second job or consulting role with a company that competes with or does business with Lumora.
- Hiring, supervising, or approving purchases from a family member or close friend.
- Serving on the board of an outside company (non-profit board service is generally fine but should still be disclosed if the organization does business with Lumora).
- Using Lumora's confidential information for personal gain, including trading securities based on non-public information.

### 7.2 How to disclose

1. Describe the situation in writing and send it to both your manager and HR (hr@lumora.example).
2. HR reviews the disclosure, may ask for more information, and records the outcome in your personnel file.
3. If a mitigation plan is needed (for example, removing you from a vendor selection), HR and your manager will agree it with you in writing.

### 7.3 Annual disclosure certification

Each January, every employee completes an annual conflict of interest disclosure certification in Workday, confirming either that they have no conflicts or that all conflicts have been disclosed. New conflicts that arise during the year must be disclosed promptly, without waiting for the January certification.

## 8. Gifts, Entertainment, and Hospitality

### 8.1 Accepting gifts

Employees may accept gifts of nominal value, up to $100 per year per source (for example, per vendor or per customer company). Employees may never accept cash or cash equivalents, such as gift cards, under any circumstances. If you receive a gift that exceeds the limit, politely return it, or give it to HR so it can be donated or shared, and let the giver know about our policy.

Never accept a gift, meal, or favor that is offered in connection with a pending decision, such as a contract award, renewal, or vendor selection, regardless of its value.

### 8.2 Giving gifts to customers

Gifts to customers are capped at $100 per person per year. Gift cards to customers require Legal pre-approval. Client entertainment must follow the limits and documentation rules in FIN-001 Expense Reimbursement Policy. Gifts or hospitality for government officials, including employees of public universities or state agencies, require Legal pre-approval regardless of value.

### 8.3 Anti-bribery

Lumora prohibits offering, promising, giving, requesting, or accepting anything of value to improperly influence a business decision. This applies to commercial partners and to government officials, and it applies whether the payment is made directly or through a third party such as a reseller or consultant.

## 9. Personal Conduct at Work

### 9.1 Dress code

Business casual dress is the standard in Lumora offices. When meeting customers, in person or on video, dress in a way that is appropriate for that client and setting. Teams may set reasonable additional expectations for events such as trade shows. Lumora will provide reasonable accommodation for religious dress and grooming practices and for disability-related needs.

### 9.2 Workplace relationships

Lumora does not prohibit personal relationships between colleagues, but they must not create conflicts of interest or affect the workplace. A romantic relationship between a manager and a direct or indirect report must be disclosed to HR. HR will work with the people involved to adjust reporting lines or decision-making so that no one is evaluating, promoting, or setting pay for a partner. Failing to disclose such a relationship is a violation of this Code.

### 9.3 Alcohol and drugs

Employees must not work while impaired by alcohol, cannabis, illegal drugs, or misused prescription medication. Alcohol may be consumed only at approved company events, and in moderation. Possession, sale, or use of illegal drugs on company premises or while conducting company business is prohibited. Employees who need help with substance use are encouraged to contact the Employee Assistance Program (EAP), described in HR-006 Employee Benefits Guide.

### 9.4 Workplace violence and weapons

Workplace violence, including threats, intimidation, and physical aggression, is prohibited. Weapons are prohibited on company premises, including offices, parking areas controlled by Lumora, and company-sponsored events, except where state law specifically requires otherwise. If you believe someone is in immediate danger, call 911 first, then notify your manager and HR.

### 9.5 Use of company property

Company equipment, systems, and accounts are provided for business purposes. Limited incidental personal use of company devices is acceptable if it does not interfere with work or violate policy. Detailed rules, including approved software and generative AI tools, are in SEC-002 IT Acceptable Use Policy.

## 10. Protecting Information

Employees must protect Lumora's confidential information and the information our customers entrust to us. Key expectations include:

- Classify and handle data according to SEC-001 (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted).
- Use company-provided 1Password for all work passwords and use MFA on all company systems.
- Report suspected security incidents, including lost or stolen devices and clicked phishing links, within 1 hour to security@lumora.example, the #sec-incidents Slack channel, or the security incident hotline at 1-512-555-0199.
- Forward privacy-related requests from individuals to privacy@lumora.example as described in LEG-001.

Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends. Nothing in this handbook prevents employees from discussing wages, hours, or working conditions, or from reporting possible legal violations to a government agency.

## 11. External Communications and Social Media

Only authorized spokespeople may speak on behalf of Lumora to the media, analysts, or investors. If you are contacted by a journalist, refer the inquiry to the Communications team. On social media, you may mention that you work at Lumora, but you must not speak on behalf of Lumora unless authorized by Communications, and you must never disclose confidential information. When you share opinions about the industry, make clear that they are your own.

## 12. Raising Concerns, Investigations, and Non-Retaliation

### 12.1 How to report

Employees can report concerns about possible misconduct, policy violations, or illegal activity through any of these channels:

- Your manager (or any other manager you trust).
- HR, by email at hr@lumora.example.
- The Ethics & Compliance hotline, at 1-800-555-0142, which is anonymous and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Managers who receive a report must forward it to HR promptly and must not attempt to investigate on their own.

### 12.2 Investigation process

1. **Intake.** HR or Ethics & Compliance logs the report and assigns an investigator who has no conflict of interest.
2. **Start.** Investigations begin within 5 business days of a report.
3. **Fact-finding.** The investigator interviews the reporter (if known), the subject, and witnesses, and reviews relevant documents and system records.
4. **Completion.** The target is to complete investigations within 30 days. Complex cases may take longer; in that case the reporter will be told the investigation is ongoing.
5. **Outcome.** Findings are documented. Corrective action, if any, is decided by HR together with the relevant leadership. The reporter is told when the matter is closed, though details may be limited to protect privacy.

Employees are required to cooperate fully and honestly with investigations and to keep the matter confidential to protect the integrity of the process.

### 12.3 Non-retaliation

Lumora has a strict non-retaliation policy. No one may be demoted, disciplined, excluded, or otherwise treated unfavorably because they raised a concern in good faith or took part in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a serious violation of this Code and will lead to discipline up to and including termination. Reports made in good faith are protected even if the concern turns out to be unfounded.

## 13. Corrective Action

Violations of this handbook or other Lumora policies may result in corrective action, which can include coaching, a written warning, a Performance Improvement Plan (described in HR-005), or termination of employment. Lumora may skip steps depending on the seriousness of the conduct. Serious misconduct, such as theft, violence, harassment, fraud, or deliberate data misuse, may result in immediate termination.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. Can I accept a $75 bottle of wine from a vendor during the holidays?**
Yes, if the total value of gifts you have received from that vendor this year, including the wine, is no more than $100, and the vendor is not part of a pending decision you are involved in. You may never accept a gift card, regardless of the amount.

**Q2. I started a side business selling photography. Do I need to disclose it?**
If it does not compete with Lumora, does not do business with Lumora, and does not use company time or equipment, it is usually not a conflict. When in doubt, disclose it in writing to your manager and HR. You will also confirm it in the January annual disclosure certification.

**Q3. Can I report a concern anonymously?**
Yes. Call the Ethics & Compliance hotline at 1-800-555-0142, which is anonymous and available 24/7. Anonymous reports are investigated in the same way as named reports, though it helps to provide as much detail as possible.

**Q4. How quickly will my report be looked at?**
Investigations begin within 5 business days of a report, with a target completion of 30 days.

**Q5. What are core hours if I work remotely in a different time zone?**
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your own local time, so a Raleigh employee is available 10:00 to 15:00 Eastern time and a remote employee in Denver is available 10:00 to 15:00 Mountain time.

**Q6. When do I get paid?**
On the 15th and the last business day of each month (24 pay periods per year).

**Q7. I am dating someone on another team. Do I need to tell HR?**
Only if one of you is the other's manager, directly or indirectly. A romantic relationship between a manager and a direct or indirect report must be disclosed to HR.

**Q8. Is alcohol allowed at the office?**
Only at approved company events, and in moderation.
