## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Lumora Systems, Inc. ("Lumora" or "the company") reimburses, how employees submit expenses, the documentation required, who approves expenses, and when employees are paid back. Its goals are to make sure employees are not out of pocket for legitimate business costs, that company money is spent responsibly, and that Lumora meets its tax, audit, and record-keeping obligations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Lumora in the United States, including employees on Hybrid and Remote work arrangements, and to any expense charged to a Lumora corporate card or paid personally and claimed for reimbursement. It covers travel-related expenses (together with FIN-002 Business Travel Policy), meals, client entertainment, mileage, home-office and internet claims, stipends paid through SAP Concur, and miscellaneous business purchases.

Purchases of software, hardware, and services above the limits set by Procurement must go through the purchasing process and are not expense reports. Software for company devices must be requested as described in SEC-002 IT Acceptable Use Policy.

## 3. Definitions

- **Business expense**: a reasonable and necessary cost incurred while performing work for Lumora.
- **Expense report**: a collection of one or more expenses submitted together in SAP Concur for approval.
- **Itemized receipt**: a receipt that shows the vendor name, date, each item purchased with its price, taxes, tip (if any), the total, and the method of payment. A credit card slip that shows only a total is not an itemized receipt.
- **Corporate card**: the Lumora-issued American Express (Amex) card.
- **Approver**: the person who must approve an expense report in SAP Concur, normally the employee's direct manager.
- **Client entertainment**: a meal or event with one or more people from a customer, prospect, or partner organization, held for a clear business purpose.
- **Final approval**: the point at which all required approvers and the Finance audit review have approved an expense report in SAP Concur.

## 4. General principles

1. Spend company money as if it were your own. Choose reasonable, cost-effective options.
2. Only business expenses are reimbursable. Personal expenses are never reimbursed, even if incurred while on a business trip.
3. You may not approve your own expenses, and you may not split one expense across multiple reports or transactions to avoid an approval threshold or receipt requirement.
4. When two or more employees share a business meal, the most senior employee present pays and submits the expense, listing all attendees.
5. Expenses must be submitted by the person who incurred them, except where an assistant has been granted delegate access in SAP Concur.
6. Submitting a false, inflated, or duplicate claim is a serious violation of HR-001 Employee Handbook & Code of Conduct and may lead to termination of employment.

## 5. Submitting an expense report

### 5.1 The system

All expenses are submitted in SAP Concur. The Concur mobile app can be used to photograph receipts at the time of purchase, which is strongly recommended. Corporate card transactions appear in Concur automatically.

### 5.2 Submission deadline

Submit each expense in SAP Concur within 30 days of incurring the expense. For a multi-day trip, the 30 days start on the last day of the trip.

Expenses submitted more than 30 days after they were incurred must include a written explanation of the delay in the report comments, and the approver may reject them. Expenses over 90 days old are not reimbursed, except with a written exception approved by the CFO. CFO exceptions are granted only for circumstances outside the employee's control, such as extended medical leave.

### 5.3 Step-by-step: creating and submitting a report

1. In SAP Concur, select **Create New Report** and enter a descriptive report name (for example, "Raleigh customer visit, March 2026").
2. Select the business purpose and cost center. Your default cost center comes from Workday.
3. Add each expense: choose the expense type, enter the date, amount, vendor, and city, and attach the receipt image.
4. For meals and entertainment, add the attendees and their companies.
5. For mileage, use the Concur mileage calculator, which applies the current rate.
6. Review any policy warnings shown by Concur and add a comment explaining any exception.
7. Select **Submit Report**. Concur routes the report to your approver(s).

### 5.4 Receipts

Itemized receipts are required for any expense of $25 or more. For expenses under $25, a receipt is recommended but not required; you must still enter the vendor, date, amount, and business purpose.

If a required receipt is lost, complete the **Missing Receipt Declaration** in SAP Concur. The declaration must describe the expense and be approved by your manager. Missing receipt declarations should be rare; repeated use will be reviewed by Finance and may result in the expense being denied.

Hotel expenses always require the itemized hotel folio, regardless of amount, so that room charges, taxes, and incidentals can be separated.

## 6. Approval process

### 6.1 Who approves

Your direct manager approves your expense reports. A single expense report with a total over $5,000 also requires approval from your Vice President (VP), after the manager has approved it.

Approvers are responsible for checking that each expense is business-related, reasonable, supported by a receipt where required, and coded to the correct cost center. Approvers should act on reports within 5 business days of receiving them. If an approver is on leave, they must set up a delegate approver in Concur.

### 6.2 Finance audit

After approval, the Finance expense team reviews reports, either fully or by sample, for compliance with this policy. Finance may return a report for correction or reject individual expenses that do not comply. Questions from Finance should be answered promptly so that payment is not delayed.

### 6.3 Rejected or returned reports

If a report is returned, correct the issue noted in the comments and resubmit it. The original submission date is used when checking the 30-day and 90-day deadlines, as long as you resubmit within 10 business days of the report being returned.

## 7. Reimbursement payment

Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days of final approval. Reimbursement is paid by direct deposit to the bank account on file for payroll in Workday. It appears as a separate line on your pay statement and is not taxable income when the expense meets this policy.

Lumora does not reimburse expenses in cash and does not issue cash advances, except for international travel to locations where cards are not widely accepted, which must be arranged in advance with expenses@lumora.example.

## 8. Meals

### 8.1 Meals while traveling

When you are on business travel that requires an overnight stay, Lumora reimburses the actual cost of your own meals up to a daily cap.

- **Domestic travel**: the daily meal cap is $75 per day. As a guideline, this is about $15 for breakfast, $20 for lunch, and $40 for dinner.
- **International travel**: the daily meal cap is $100 per day.

The daily cap is the limit, not an allowance. You may spend less on one meal and more on another within the same day, but amounts above the daily cap are not reimbursed and unused amounts do not carry over to another day. Meals already provided (for example, by a conference, hotel, or airline) should not be claimed. On travel days you may claim the meals actually eaten while traveling. These meal caps are also the per diem rules referenced in FIN-002 Business Travel Policy.

Tips for meals are reimbursable as part of the meal cost and should be reasonable, generally 15 to 20 percent.

### 8.2 Local meals

Meals are not reimbursed when you are working in your home city or office, except for client entertainment (Section 9) or team events approved in advance by a manager with a budget.

### 8.3 Alcohol

Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of client entertainment and must be reasonable. Alcohol is never reimbursed for solo meals or meals with only Lumora employees, unless the event is an approved company event under HR-001.

## 9. Client entertainment

Client entertainment is reimbursable up to $100 per person, including all attendees from Lumora and the client, and including tax and tip. For each client entertainment expense you must record:

- the names of all attendees,
- the company each attendee represents, and
- the business purpose of the meal or event (for example, "Quarterly business review with renewal discussion").

Entertainment above $100 per person requires pre-approval from your VP. Entertainment must not be lavish or create the appearance of improper influence. Entertainment of government officials requires prior approval from Legal.

## 10. Gifts to customers

Gifts to customers are capped at $100 per person per year. Gift cards to customers require pre-approval from Legal before purchase, because they can be treated as cash equivalents. Record the recipient and business purpose for every gift. For gifts that employees receive from third parties, see HR-001 Employee Handbook & Code of Conduct.

## 11. Mileage and ground transportation

### 11.1 Personal vehicle mileage

When you use your personal vehicle for business, Lumora reimburses mileage at the current IRS standard mileage rate. The rate in effect is published in SAP Concur and is updated whenever the IRS changes it. Mileage reimbursement covers fuel, wear, and insurance, so fuel receipts are not reimbursed separately for personal vehicles. Parking fees and tolls incurred on business trips are reimbursable in addition to mileage.

Commuting is not reimbursable. Commuting means travel between your home and your regular work location, including your assigned office on Hybrid in-office days. If you drive from home directly to a customer site or another business location, you may claim only the miles that exceed your normal commute.

### 11.2 Rideshare, taxi, and rental cars

Rideshare and taxi rides for business purposes are reimbursable. Rental cars must follow FIN-002 Business Travel Policy, including the compact or midsize class rule. Fuel for rental cars is reimbursable; refuel before returning the car to avoid rental company fuel charges.

## 12. Corporate card (Amex)

### 12.1 Eligibility

A corporate card is issued to employees who travel 4 or more times per year, or to other employees at their manager's request. Request a card through the expenses team at expenses@lumora.example.

### 12.2 Rules for use

- Use the corporate card for business expenses only. Personal charges on the corporate card are forbidden, even if you intend to repay them.
- If a personal charge is made by mistake, notify expenses@lumora.example immediately and repay the amount through Concur.
- Reconcile corporate card transactions in SAP Concur monthly, attaching receipts and business purposes.
- Report a lost or stolen card to Amex and to expenses@lumora.example immediately.
- Cards are cancelled when employment ends; outstanding transactions must be reconciled before your last day, as described in HR-008.

Misuse of the corporate card may result in cancellation of the card and disciplinary action.

## 13. Home office, internet, and stipend claims

Several benefits under other policies are paid as expense claims through SAP Concur and follow the submission, receipt, and approval rules in this policy:

- The one-time $500 home-office equipment stipend and the $50 per month internet reimbursement for Hybrid and Remote employees (HR-004 Remote & Hybrid Work Policy).
- The $50 per month wellness stipend (HR-006 Employee Benefits Guide).
- The $1,500 per calendar year learning and development stipend (HR-006 Employee Benefits Guide).

Use the specific expense type for each benefit in Concur so that the claim is counted against the correct limit.

## 14. Non-reimbursable expenses

The following are never reimbursed:

- Traffic violations, parking tickets, and other fines.
- Personal entertainment, such as movies, in-room entertainment, sporting events, or sightseeing not connected with client entertainment.
- Seat upgrades or class upgrades on flights, trains, or hotels that were not pre-approved.
- Hotel minibar charges.
- Travel and expenses for a spouse, partner, family member, or companion.
- Gym, spa, or fitness fees while on a trip.
- Lost or stolen personal items, including luggage, clothing, and personal electronics.
- Alcohol on solo meals or meals with only Lumora employees (see Section 8.3).
- Commuting costs (see Section 11.1).
- Personal grooming, toiletries, clothing, and personal medications.
- Late payment fees and interest on personal credit cards.
- Donations to charities or political organizations, which must go through the company giving process instead.

## 15. Records and retention

Expense reports and receipts are financial records and are retained for 7 years under LEG-001 Data Privacy & Protection Policy. Receipt images stored in SAP Concur are the official copy; employees do not need to keep paper originals after the report has been approved. Do not include unnecessary personal data, such as full card numbers, in receipt images.

## 16. Frequently asked questions

### Q1. How long do I have to submit an expense?

Submit each expense in SAP Concur within 30 days. Expenses over 90 days old are not reimbursed unless the CFO approves an exception.

### Q2. When do I need a receipt?

An itemized receipt is required for every expense of $25 or more, and the itemized hotel folio is always required for hotel stays.

### Q3. Who approves my expense report?

Your direct manager. If a single report totals more than $5,000, your VP must also approve it.

### Q4. When will I be paid back?

Within 10 business days of final approval, by direct deposit to your payroll bank account.

### Q5. What is the daily meal limit when traveling?

$75 per day for domestic travel (about $15 breakfast, $20 lunch, $40 dinner) and $100 per day for international travel.

### Q6. Can I expense a glass of wine with dinner on a business trip?

Only if the dinner is client entertainment. Alcohol is never reimbursed for solo meals.

### Q7. How much can I spend on a client dinner?

Up to $100 per person. List all attendees, their companies, and the business purpose.

### Q8. Can I claim mileage for driving to the office on my Hybrid days?

No. Commuting between home and your regular work location is not reimbursable.
