## 1. Purpose

This guide explains the benefits Lumora Systems, Inc. ("Lumora") offers to its employees: health insurance, retirement savings, insurance protection, stipends, and support programs. It tells employees who is eligible, how and when to enroll, how much Lumora contributes, and how to get help.

This guide is a summary. The official plan documents and Summary Plan Descriptions (SPDs) control the details of each benefit plan. If this guide and a plan document differ, the plan document controls. Copies of all plan documents are available in Workday under "Benefits" and from benefits@lumora.example.

## 2. Scope

This guide applies to all full-time employees of Lumora working in the United States, including employees in the Austin and Raleigh offices and remote employees. A full-time employee is one regularly scheduled to work 30 or more hours per week.

Part-time employees scheduled for fewer than 30 hours per week, interns, temporary employees and contractors are not eligible for the benefits in this guide unless a specific section says otherwise or the law requires it. Part-time employees remain eligible for the 401(k) plan as described in section 7.

Paid time off, holidays and leaves of absence are not covered here. See HR-002 and HR-003.

## 3. Definitions

- Benefits year: The calendar year, January 1 through December 31. Elections made during open enrollment apply for the full benefits year.
- Dependent: An employee's legal spouse or domestic partner, and children up to age 26 (including stepchildren, adopted children and children placed for adoption). Disabled children over 26 may qualify under plan rules.
- Employee premium: The monthly cost of coverage for the employee alone.
- Dependent premium: The additional monthly cost of adding dependents to coverage.
- Qualifying life event (QLE): A change in circumstances that allows an employee to change benefit elections outside open enrollment, such as marriage, divorce, birth or adoption, loss of other coverage, or a move that changes plan availability.
- HSA: Health Savings Account, a tax-advantaged savings account paired with a high-deductible health plan.
- Vesting: The point at which an employee owns employer contributions to a retirement account.
- EAP: Employee Assistance Program, a free and confidential support service.

## 4. Eligibility and Enrollment

### 4.1 When coverage starts

Full-time employees (30 or more hours per week) are eligible for benefits from their first day of employment. Coverage elected during the new-hire enrollment window is effective retroactive to the first day of employment.

### 4.2 New-hire enrollment window

New employees must enroll within 30 days of their hire date. Enrollment is completed in Workday under "Benefits > Benefit Elections." Employees who do not enroll within 30 days receive the following default coverage:

- Company-paid life insurance, AD&D, short-term disability and long-term disability (automatic, no election needed).
- 401(k) auto-enrollment at 3% of salary (see section 7).
- No medical, dental or vision coverage.

An employee who misses the 30-day window can next enroll during open enrollment, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

### 4.3 Open enrollment

Open enrollment takes place every November for coverage in the following calendar year. During open enrollment, employees can add, drop or change medical, dental and vision plans, add or remove dependents, and change HSA and FSA elections. HR announces the exact dates by email and in Slack in October. If an employee takes no action, their current medical, dental and vision elections roll over to the next year, but HSA and FSA contribution elections must be made again each year.

### 4.4 Qualifying life events

Employees can change their elections outside open enrollment only if they have a qualifying life event. Changes must be requested in Workday within 30 days of the event, with supporting documentation (for example, a marriage certificate or birth certificate). Changes must be consistent with the event; for example, the birth of a child allows the employee to add the child and switch medical plans, but not to drop vision coverage for unrelated reasons.

Common qualifying life events:

- Marriage, divorce, legal separation, or start or end of a domestic partnership.
- Birth, adoption or placement for adoption of a child.
- Death of a dependent.
- A dependent gaining or losing other health coverage.
- A move outside the service area of the employee's current plan (for example, an HMO).
- A change from part-time to full-time status, or the reverse.

### 4.5 Dependent verification

When adding dependents, employees must upload proof of eligibility in Workday within 30 days. Dependents without verified documents are removed from coverage.

## 5. Health Insurance

### 5.1 Medical plans

Lumora offers three medical plans. All three plans include preventive care at no cost when using in-network providers, prescription drug coverage, and mental health coverage on the same terms as medical coverage.

- PPO (Preferred Provider Organization): The broadest network and the ability to see out-of-network providers at a higher cost. No referrals needed for specialists. Higher premiums, lower deductible.
- HDHP with HSA (High-Deductible Health Plan): Lower premiums and a higher deductible. Pairs with a Health Savings Account and an employer HSA contribution (section 5.2). Good for employees who want to save for future health costs.
- HMO (Health Maintenance Organization): Lower out-of-pocket costs but a narrower network. Requires a primary care physician and referrals for specialists. No out-of-network coverage except emergencies. Availability depends on where the employee lives.

### 5.2 Lumora's premium contributions

For all three medical plans, Lumora pays 90% of the employee premium and 75% of dependent premiums. The employee pays the remaining 10% of the employee premium and 25% of dependent premiums through pre-tax payroll deductions, spread across the 24 semi-monthly pay periods.

Example: If the total monthly PPO premium for an employee alone is $600, Lumora pays $540 and the employee pays $60 per month ($30 per paycheck). If adding a spouse costs an additional $800 per month, Lumora pays $600 of that and the employee pays $200.

Premium rates for each plan and coverage tier are published in Workday during open enrollment.

### 5.3 Health Savings Account (HSA)

Employees enrolled in the HDHP can open an HSA through Lumora's HSA provider. Lumora contributes $750 per year for individual coverage and $1,500 per year for family coverage (any coverage tier above employee-only). The employer contribution is deposited in equal amounts each pay period. Employees who enroll mid-year receive a prorated employer contribution.

Employees can add their own pre-tax contributions through payroll, up to the IRS annual limit minus Lumora's contribution. The HSA belongs to the employee and stays with them if they leave Lumora.

### 5.4 Flexible Spending Accounts (FSAs)

Employees enrolled in the PPO or HMO may contribute to a health care FSA. Employees in the HDHP may contribute to a limited-purpose FSA for dental and vision expenses only. All employees may contribute to a dependent care FSA for child or elder care. FSA contribution limits follow IRS rules. FSA funds not used by the plan deadline are forfeited, except for any carryover amount allowed by the plan document.

### 5.5 Dental insurance

Lumora offers one dental plan covering preventive care, basic services and major services, plus orthodontia for children. Lumora pays 100% of the employee premium for dental. Employees pay for dependent dental coverage through payroll deductions.

### 5.6 Vision insurance

Lumora offers one vision plan covering an annual eye exam and an allowance for glasses or contact lenses. Lumora pays 100% of the employee premium for vision. Employees pay for dependent vision coverage through payroll deductions.

## 6. Insurance Protection

### 6.1 Life insurance

Lumora provides basic life insurance equal to 2 times annual base salary, paid entirely by the company. Employees do not need to enroll; coverage is automatic from the first day of employment. Employees should name a beneficiary in Workday.

Employees may buy supplemental life insurance for themselves, their spouse or domestic partner, and their children at group rates. Amounts above the guaranteed issue limit require evidence of insurability.

### 6.2 Accidental death and dismemberment (AD&D)

Lumora provides AD&D insurance equal to 2 times annual base salary, also company-paid. AD&D pays in addition to life insurance if death or certain serious injuries result from an accident.

### 6.3 Short-term disability (STD)

Short-term disability pays 60% of base pay for up to 12 weeks when an employee cannot work because of a non-work-related illness, injury, surgery or pregnancy. STD is company-paid. Claims are filed with the disability carrier, and HR coordinates STD with any leave under HR-002, including parental leave and FMLA. Sick days from the sick leave bank in HR-002 may be used during the waiting period before STD benefits start.

### 6.4 Long-term disability (LTD)

Long-term disability pays 60% of base pay, up to a maximum of $15,000 per month, if a disability lasts beyond the short-term disability period. LTD is company-paid. Because the premium is paid by Lumora, LTD benefits may be taxable income.

## 7. Retirement: 401(k) Plan

### 7.1 Eligibility and auto-enrollment

All employees, including part-time employees, may participate in the Lumora 401(k) plan from their first day. New employees are auto-enrolled at a pre-tax contribution of 3% of salary unless they choose a different rate or opt out in Workday within 30 days of hire. Employees can change their contribution rate at any time; changes take effect in the next available pay period.

Employees can contribute on a pre-tax basis, a Roth (after-tax) basis, or a combination, up to the IRS annual limit. Employees aged 50 and over may make catch-up contributions.

### 7.2 Company match

Lumora matches 100% of the first 4% of salary an employee contributes, plus 50% of the next 2%. The maximum company match is therefore 5% of salary, reached when the employee contributes 6% or more.

| Employee contribution | Lumora match |
|---|---|
| 0% | 0% |
| 3% (auto-enrollment default) | 3% |
| 4% | 4% |
| 5% | 4.5% |
| 6% or more | 5% (maximum) |

The match is calculated and deposited each pay period. Eligible pay for the match includes base salary and the annual bonus.

### 7.3 Vesting

Vesting is immediate. Employees own 100% of both their own contributions and Lumora's matching contributions from the day they are made.

### 7.4 Investment and rollovers

Employees choose investments from the plan's fund menu. If no choice is made, contributions go into a target-date fund based on the employee's age. Employees may roll eligible balances from a previous employer's plan into the Lumora 401(k).

## 8. Stipends and Allowances

### 8.1 Learning and development stipend

Each employee receives a learning and development (L&D) stipend of $1,500 per calendar year. The stipend can be used for courses, certifications and exam fees, conferences, and books related to the employee's current role or career development at Lumora.

- Manager approval is required before the expense is incurred.
- Claims are submitted in SAP Concur under the "Learning & Development" category, following the receipt and timing rules in FIN-001.
- Unused stipend does not carry over to the next year and is not paid out.
- New hires receive the full $1,500 in their hire year.
- Travel to an approved conference is booked under FIN-002 and paid from the department travel budget, not the L&D stipend, unless the manager decides otherwise.

### 8.2 Wellness stipend

Each employee receives a wellness stipend of $50 per month, claimed through SAP Concur. Eligible expenses include gym memberships, fitness classes, fitness apps, meditation apps, and athletic equipment. Unused monthly amounts do not roll forward. The wellness stipend is taxable income.

### 8.3 Home-office and internet stipends

Hybrid and Remote employees receive a one-time $500 home-office stipend and a $50 per month internet reimbursement. These are described in HR-004 and are listed here only for completeness.

## 9. Support Programs

### 9.1 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

The EAP is free and confidential for employees and members of their household. It provides 6 counseling sessions per issue per year with a licensed counselor, in person, by video or by phone. The EAP also offers legal and financial consultations, help finding child and elder care, and a 24/7 crisis line. Lumora does not receive information about who uses the EAP. Contact details are in Workday under "Benefits > EAP."

### 9.2 Commuter benefits

Employees can set aside pre-tax money for transit passes and qualified parking, up to IRS monthly limits. Elections can be made or changed monthly in Workday; open enrollment is not needed.

## 10. Leaving Lumora and Changes in Status

- Medical, dental and vision coverage end on the last day of the month in which employment ends.
- COBRA continuation information is sent within 14 days of separation, as described in HR-008. COBRA allows employees and dependents to continue coverage at their own cost for a limited period.
- Life, AD&D, STD and LTD coverage end on the last day of employment. Some life insurance may be converted or ported to an individual policy by applying with the carrier within the carrier's deadline.
- 401(k) balances, including the company match, belong to the employee and may be left in the plan, rolled over, or withdrawn under plan rules.
- HSA balances belong to the employee.
- If an employee's hours drop below 30 per week, they lose eligibility for medical, dental, vision and insurance benefits at the end of that month, and receive COBRA information.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much of my medical premium does Lumora pay?
A: Lumora pays 90% of the employee premium and 75% of dependent premiums for all three medical plans (PPO, HDHP with HSA, and HMO).

Q: What is the maximum 401(k) match?
A: 5% of salary. Lumora matches 100% of the first 4% you contribute and 50% of the next 2%, so contributing 6% gets the full match. The match vests immediately.

Q: How long do I have to enroll in benefits after I start?
A: 30 days from your hire date. Coverage is effective from your first day.

Q: I got married in March. Can I add my spouse to my medical plan?
A: Yes. Marriage is a qualifying life event. Make the change in Workday within 30 days of the marriage and upload a marriage certificate.

Q: How much does Lumora put into my HSA?
A: $750 per year for individual coverage, or $1,500 per year for family coverage, if you are enrolled in the HDHP.

Q: Can I carry over unused learning and development money?
A: No. The $1,500 L&D stipend is per calendar year and unused amounts are forfeited.

Q: Do I pay anything for dental and vision?
A: Lumora pays 100% of the employee premium for dental and vision. You pay only for any dependents you add.

Q: How many EAP counseling sessions do I get?
A: Six free, confidential sessions per issue per year.
