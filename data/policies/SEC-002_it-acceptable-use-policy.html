1. Purpose

This IT Acceptable Use Policy explains how Lumora Systems, Inc. ("Lumora") devices, accounts, networks, software, and online services may and may not be used. Its goal is to protect Lumora, our customers, and our people from security, legal, and reputational harm while allowing employees to work productively and flexibly.

This policy supplements SEC-001 Information Security Policy, which sets the core security controls (data classification, passwords, MFA, encryption, patching, and incident reporting). Where this policy and SEC-001 overlap, both apply. Questions about personal data are covered in LEG-001 Data Privacy & Protection Policy.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All employees in every work arrangement (On-site, Hybrid, and Remote), and all contractors, interns, and temporary workers with Lumora accounts.
  • All company-owned devices, including laptops, monitors, docking stations, phones, and accessories.
  • All company accounts and systems, including email, Slack, Workday, SAP Concur, Navan, ServiceNow, source code repositories, cloud platforms, and AI tools.
  • Personal devices when they are used to access company email or Slack through Microsoft Intune app protection.
  • Company networks, the GlobalProtect VPN, and office Wi-Fi.

3. Definitions

  • Company systems: Any device, account, application, network, or service owned, licensed, or managed by Lumora.
  • Incidental personal use: Brief, occasional personal use of a company device or account that does not interfere with work and does not break any policy, such as checking a personal news site during a break.
  • Lumora Self Service portal: The company application catalog from which employees can install pre-approved software on company laptops without an IT ticket.
  • Approved AI tools: The generative AI tools Lumora has approved for work use: Lumora's enterprise AI assistant and GitHub Copilot Business.
  • Unapproved AI tool: Any generative AI service not listed above, including free or consumer versions of public chatbots, browser extensions, and AI features in third-party apps that have not been approved.
  • EDR: Endpoint detection and response software. Lumora uses CrowdStrike.

4. General Use of Company Devices and Systems

4.1 Business use

Company devices and systems are provided for business purposes. You are responsible for all activity performed with your accounts and devices.

4.2 Incidental personal use

Limited incidental personal use of company devices is permitted if it does not interfere with your work or anyone else's, does not consume significant resources, and does not violate this or any other Lumora policy. Examples of acceptable incidental use include reading the news, making a personal appointment, or checking a personal bank balance during a break. Personal files should be kept to a minimum; Lumora does not back up personal files and is not responsible for their loss. Personal use never creates an expectation of privacy (see Section 9).

4.3 Caring for equipment

  • Keep company devices physically secure and in good condition. Report damage, loss, or theft as described in SEC-001 (within 1 hour for lost or stolen devices).
  • Do not let family members, friends, or anyone else use your company laptop.
  • Do not remove asset tags or attempt to repair hardware yourself; open a ServiceNow ticket instead.
  • Return all equipment when you leave Lumora as described in HR-008 Onboarding & Offboarding Procedures.

5. Software Installation and Licensing

5.1 Approved sources

Software may be installed on company devices only from the Lumora Self Service portal or after approval through a ServiceNow request. This applies to desktop applications, browser extensions, command-line tools, plug-ins, and trial versions.

5.2 Requesting new software

  1. Check the Lumora Self Service portal first. If the software is listed, install it from there.
  2. If it is not listed, submit a software request in the Lumora Help ServiceNow portal with the software name, vendor, business purpose, and the type of data it will handle.
  3. IT and Security review the request. Software that will process Confidential or Restricted data, or personal data, may require a vendor security review and, for personal data, a privacy impact assessment under LEG-001.
  4. Install the software only after the request is approved.

5.3 Licensing

Pirated, cracked, or unlicensed software is never allowed on company devices. Do not use personal licenses for company work unless IT has approved it, and do not share license keys outside of approved channels.

6. Generative AI Tools

6.1 Approved tools

Only approved generative AI tools may be used for Lumora work. The approved tools are Lumora's enterprise AI assistant and GitHub Copilot Business. These tools are covered by enterprise agreements that protect Lumora data and prevent it from being used to train public models.

6.2 Data rules for AI

Never put Confidential or Restricted data into unapproved or public AI tools. This includes customer data, personal data, source code, credentials, contracts, product roadmaps, and unreleased financial results. Data classifications are defined in SEC-001.

6.3 Human review

AI output must be reviewed by a human before it is used. You are responsible for checking AI-generated text, code, and analysis for accuracy, security issues, bias, and licensing concerns before you share it, ship it, or rely on it. AI-generated code is subject to the same code review requirements as code written by a person.

6.4 Requesting a new AI tool

To propose another AI tool, submit a ServiceNow request. Security and Legal will review it. Until a tool is approved, treat it as unapproved.

8. Email, Chat, and Collaboration

8.1 Email

  • Company email must not be auto-forwarded to personal accounts. Do not set up forwarding rules to external addresses.
  • Do not send Restricted data by unencrypted email. Use approved secure sharing methods.
  • Double-check recipients before sending, especially when autocomplete suggests an external address. Sending data to the wrong recipient must be reported as an incident under SEC-001.
  • Report suspicious emails using the "Report Phishing" button.

8.2 Slack

  • Slack is the company chat platform. Keep business conversations in Lumora Slack, not in personal messaging apps.
  • Do not post Restricted data, such as passwords, API keys, or customer PII, in Slack channels or direct messages.
  • Use the correct channel for the audience. External shared channels with customers or vendors must be treated as external communication.

8.3 File sharing

Share files using company-approved storage and collaboration tools. Do not upload company files to personal cloud storage. When sharing externally, use the most restrictive link setting that works, and remove access when it is no longer needed.

8.4 Video meetings and recordings

  • Use company-approved video conferencing tools for Lumora meetings.
  • Tell participants before you record a meeting, and record only when there is a business reason. Recordings and AI-generated transcripts are company records and must be stored in approved company storage, not on personal devices.
  • Be aware of what is visible on screen and in the background when sharing your screen. Close windows that show Confidential or Restricted data before you start sharing.
  • Do not join confidential meetings from public places where others can overhear.

8.5 Printing and office equipment

  • Print Confidential or Restricted documents only when necessary, collect them from the printer immediately, and dispose of them in locked shredding bins.
  • Do not use hotel business centers or public print shops for Confidential or Restricted documents.
  • Shared office equipment, such as conference room displays and printers, must not be reconfigured or connected to personal accounts. Report faulty equipment through the Lumora Help ServiceNow portal.

9. Monitoring and Privacy on Company Systems

Lumora may monitor the use of company systems, including devices, email, Slack, network traffic, and cloud services, to protect security, investigate incidents, comply with law, and enforce policy. Monitoring is performed by authorized staff only and in accordance with applicable law.

Users have no expectation of privacy on company devices or company systems. This includes personal content created, stored, or sent using company devices or accounts. On personal phones enrolled in Microsoft Intune app protection, Lumora manages only the company apps and company data; it does not access personal photos, personal messages, or personal apps.

Personal data collected through monitoring is handled according to LEG-001 Data Privacy & Protection Policy.

10. Social Media and Public Communication

  • Do not speak on behalf of Lumora on social media, in public forums, or to the press unless you are authorized by Communications.
  • Never disclose confidential information, including customer names that have not been approved for public reference, product roadmaps, financial information, or security details.
  • When you mention your work at Lumora in a personal post, make clear that your views are your own.
  • Refer media inquiries to the Communications team.
  • Be respectful; the standards in HR-001 Employee Handbook & Code of Conduct and HR-007 Anti-Harassment, Equal Opportunity & Workplace Conduct Policy apply online as well.

11. Remote Use and Networks

When working outside a Lumora office, connect through the GlobalProtect VPN. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected. Do not connect company laptops to untrusted USB devices or charging kiosks; USB mass storage is blocked by default as described in SEC-001. Requirements for working from another state or another country are described in HR-004 Remote & Hybrid Work Policy.

12. Reporting Violations

If you see a possible violation of this policy, report it to security@lumora.example or in #sec-incidents. Concerns about misconduct may also be reported to your manager, to HR at hr@lumora.example, or anonymously to the Ethics & Compliance hotline at 1-800-555-0142. Lumora strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

13. Enforcement

Violations may result in removal of software, suspension of system access, and disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract. Lumora may report illegal activity to law enforcement. Exceptions to this policy must be requested through ServiceNow and approved by Security.

14. Frequently Asked Questions

14.1 Can I use my work laptop to shop online during lunch?

Yes. Limited incidental personal use is allowed if it does not interfere with your work or violate policy. Remember that you have no expectation of privacy on company devices.

14.2 Can I paste customer data into a public chatbot to summarize it?

No. Customer data is Restricted. Never put Confidential or Restricted data into unapproved or public AI tools. Use Lumora's enterprise AI assistant instead, and review the output before using it.

14.3 Which AI tools am I allowed to use?

Lumora's enterprise AI assistant and GitHub Copilot Business. Any other tool needs approval through a ServiceNow request.

14.4 How do I install a tool that is not in the Lumora Self Service portal?

Submit a software request in the Lumora Help ServiceNow portal. Install it only after approval.

14.5 Can I forward my work email to my personal Gmail so I can read it on my phone?

No. Auto-forwarding company email to personal accounts is prohibited. Instead, enroll your phone in Microsoft Intune app protection and use the approved mail app.

14.6 CrowdStrike is slowing down my build. Can I turn it off temporarily?

No. Disabling security tools is prohibited. Open a ServiceNow ticket so IT and Security can investigate performance issues.

14.7 Can I post about our new product on LinkedIn?

You may share content that Lumora has already made public. Do not share unreleased or confidential information, and do not speak on behalf of Lumora unless Communications has authorized you.
