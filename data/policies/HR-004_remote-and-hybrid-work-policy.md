## 1. Purpose

Lumora Systems, Inc. ("Lumora" or "the company") believes that great work happens both in person and at a distance. This policy explains the work arrangements Lumora offers, how employees request and keep an arrangement, what the company provides to support people working outside the office, and the expectations that apply wherever work is done. It aims to give employees flexibility while protecting collaboration, customer commitments, information security, and the company's legal and tax obligations.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to every Lumora employee based in the United States, whether assigned to the Austin, Texas headquarters, the Raleigh, North Carolina office, or approved to work remotely from another US state. It covers where employees work, when they must be available, how they connect to company systems from outside the office, and the stipends available to support home workspaces.

This policy does not change an employee's at-will status, job duties, compensation, or eligibility for benefits. Contractors and temporary agency workers follow the terms of their contracts, although they must still meet the security requirements in SEC-001 and SEC-002 when accessing Lumora systems.

## 3. Definitions

- **Work arrangement**: the official classification of where an employee is expected to work, recorded on the employee's profile in Workday. There are three arrangements: On-site, Hybrid, and Remote.
- **Office**: either Lumora office location, Austin (headquarters) or Raleigh.
- **Anchor days**: the days of the week on which Hybrid employees are required to be in the office. Lumora's anchor days are Tuesday and Wednesday.
- **Core collaboration hours**: 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time, during which employees must be reachable.
- **Home state**: the US state recorded in Workday as the employee's primary work location, used for payroll tax withholding and employment law purposes.
- **Temporary work location**: any place other than the employee's office or recorded home address from which the employee works for a limited period.
- **VPN**: Lumora's company virtual private network, GlobalProtect.
- **Sanctioned country**: a country or region subject to comprehensive US trade sanctions or embargoes, from which work is prohibited.

## 4. Work arrangements

Lumora offers three work arrangements. Each employee has exactly one arrangement at a time.

### 4.1 On-site

On-site employees work from a Lumora office every scheduled workday. On-site is used for roles that depend on physical presence, such as facilities, office operations, hardware lab support, and some IT service desk positions. On-site employees may occasionally work from home with manager approval (for example, while waiting for a home repair), but this does not change their arrangement.

### 4.2 Hybrid (default)

Hybrid is the default work arrangement for all office-assigned roles. Unless an employee has an approved On-site or Remote arrangement, they are Hybrid.

Hybrid employees work in the office 3 days per week. Tuesday and Wednesday are required anchor days for everyone, and each employee chooses one additional in-office day each week. The remaining two days may be worked from home or another approved location.

Rules for the third day:

- The employee chooses the third day in agreement with their manager. Teams are encouraged, but not required, to choose the same third day so they can meet in person.
- The third day may vary from week to week, as long as the employee keeps their team informed through their Slack status or the team calendar.
- A week that contains a company holiday or the employee's approved PTO reduces the in-office requirement by one day for each affected workday. For example, if an employee is on PTO on Wednesday, only two in-office days are expected that week.

If an anchor day cannot be worked in the office because of illness, a family emergency, severe weather, or business travel, the employee should tell their manager as early as possible. Occasional exceptions are expected and do not require formal approval.

### 4.3 Remote

Remote employees work primarily from an approved home location and are not expected to come into an office on a regular schedule. Remote arrangements are approved only where the role can be performed effectively without regular in-person presence.

A fully Remote arrangement requires approval from the employee's Vice President (VP) and sign-off from HR. Every Remote arrangement is reviewed annually. Remote employees are expected to travel to an office or team gathering when asked by their manager, for example for quarterly planning, onboarding new team members, or customer events. Travel for these events is business travel and follows FIN-002 Business Travel Policy and FIN-001 Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## 5. Requesting or changing a work arrangement

### 5.1 How to request a Remote arrangement

1. Discuss the request with your manager. Explain how the role will be performed remotely and how you will stay connected to your team.
2. Your manager submits a "Work Arrangement Change" request in Workday on your behalf, including the business justification and the proposed home address and state.
3. The request routes to your VP for approval.
4. After VP approval, HR reviews the request for legal, tax, and payroll considerations (for example, whether Lumora is registered as an employer in your state) and gives final sign-off.
5. HR confirms the outcome in writing and updates your work arrangement in Workday. The arrangement starts on the date stated in the confirmation, not before.

### 5.2 Changing from Remote or On-site to Hybrid

Requests to move to the Hybrid arrangement need manager approval and are recorded by HR in Workday. Employees moving to Hybrid must live within a reasonable commuting distance of an office.

### 5.3 Annual review of Remote arrangements

Each Remote arrangement is reviewed once a year by the employee's manager and VP, normally alongside the annual performance cycle described in HR-005. The review considers whether the role still suits remote work, the employee's performance and responsiveness, and business needs. If the company decides to end a Remote arrangement for business reasons, HR will give the employee reasonable advance notice and discuss options such as relocation support or a transition period. Ending a Remote arrangement is not a disciplinary action unless stated in writing.

### 5.4 Arrangements are not guaranteed

Work arrangements are a business decision. A previous approval, or an arrangement held by a colleague, does not create a right to the same arrangement. Requests are assessed consistently and without regard to any protected characteristic, as required by HR-007.

## 6. Availability and working hours

### 6.1 Core collaboration hours

All employees, regardless of arrangement, must be reachable during core collaboration hours of 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time. "Reachable" means responding to Slack messages and calls within a reasonable time, attending scheduled meetings, and keeping your Slack status and calendar up to date.

Outside core hours, employees and managers agree on schedules that suit the team and the work. The standard workweek is 40 hours.

### 6.2 Non-exempt employees

Non-exempt (hourly) employees working outside the office must record all hours worked accurately in Workday, take meal and rest breaks as required by state law, and not perform work "off the clock," such as answering messages after logging off. Overtime must be approved by the manager in advance where practical, but all overtime worked will be paid.

## 7. Home workspace and stipends

### 7.1 Workspace expectations

Employees working from home must have a workspace that is safe, reasonably quiet, and suitable for confidential conversations. Screens should not be visible to other household members or visitors when Confidential or Restricted information is displayed (see SEC-001 for data classification).

Work-related injuries that occur while working at home during working hours should be reported to HR in the same way as injuries in the office.

### 7.2 Home-office equipment stipend

Hybrid and Remote employees are eligible for a one-time home-office stipend of $500 for equipment. The stipend must be used within the first 90 days of employment or, for an existing employee, within the first 90 days after moving into a Hybrid or Remote arrangement. Each employee may receive the equipment stipend only once during their employment.

Eligible items include a desk, an ergonomic chair, a monitor, a keyboard and mouse, a headset, a webcam, a desk lamp, and cable management accessories. Ineligible items include furniture not used for work, decorative items, personal computers or tablets (Lumora provides laptops), and ongoing subscriptions.

To claim the stipend, submit the itemized receipts in SAP Concur under the expense type "Home Office Equipment." The claim follows the submission, receipt, and approval rules in FIN-001 Expense Reimbursement Policy. Equipment bought with the stipend belongs to the employee.

Company-issued equipment, such as laptops and any loaned monitors, remains Lumora property and must be returned when employment ends, as described in HR-008.

### 7.3 Monthly internet reimbursement

Hybrid and Remote employees may claim $50 per month toward home internet service. Submit the monthly claim in SAP Concur under the expense type "Home Internet" with a copy of the bill or proof of payment. Claims follow the FIN-001 submission deadlines. On-site employees are not eligible.

The internet reimbursement is separate from the wellness stipend described in HR-006 Employee Benefits Guide; one does not reduce the other.

## 8. Working from other locations

### 8.1 Working from another US state

Short stays in another US state, such as working from a family member's home for a week, are allowed with the manager's knowledge. However, working from another US state for more than 30 days in a calendar year requires HR approval, because extended work in another state can create payroll tax registration and employment law obligations for Lumora.

To request approval, submit a "Temporary Work Location" request in Workday at least 10 business days before the stay, listing the address, state, and dates. HR may approve, approve with conditions, or decline the request. If an employee plans to move permanently to another state, they must request a change of home state through Workday before moving; a permanent move without approval may affect the employee's work arrangement.

### 8.2 Working from another country

Lumora allows employees to work temporarily from outside the United States for up to 20 working days per calendar year. This is intended for occasions such as extending a family visit, not for relocating.

Requirements:

- The request must be approved by both the employee's manager and HR.
- The request must be submitted in Workday at least 10 business days before the first day of work abroad.
- Work from sanctioned countries is prohibited and will not be approved. HR maintains the current list of prohibited countries.
- Some countries restrict the use of encryption or VPN software, or require work permits even for short stays. HR and Security may decline a request for these reasons.
- The employee must follow all security requirements in Section 9, use only company-issued devices, and stay reachable during agreed working hours.
- Days spent on business travel approved under FIN-002 do not count toward the 20-day limit.

Unused international remote work days do not carry over to the next calendar year. Working abroad without approval, or beyond the 20-day limit, is a policy violation.

## 9. Security requirements outside the office

The information security rules in SEC-001 and SEC-002 apply wherever you work. Key requirements for remote and hybrid work:

- **VPN**: Employees must connect through the company VPN, GlobalProtect, whenever they are working on any network other than the Lumora office network, including their home network.
- **Public Wi-Fi**: Public Wi-Fi (for example, in airports, hotels, and cafes) may be used only through the VPN. If the VPN cannot connect, use a personal mobile hotspot instead.
- **Devices**: Use only Lumora-issued laptops for company work. Personal phones may access email and Slack only if enrolled in Microsoft Intune app protection as described in SEC-001.
- **Screen lock**: Lock your screen whenever you step away. Screens also lock automatically after 5 minutes idle.
- **Printing**: Avoid printing Confidential or Restricted information at home. If printing is unavoidable, store printouts securely and shred them when no longer needed.
- **Home router**: Change the default administrator password on your home router and keep its firmware up to date.
- **Lost or stolen devices**: Report them to Security within 1 hour, as required by SEC-001, using security@lumora.example, the #sec-incidents Slack channel, or the security incident hotline 1-512-555-0199.

For technical problems, open a ticket in the Lumora Help portal (ServiceNow) or email it-help@lumora.example.

## 10. Roles and responsibilities

- **Employees** follow their approved arrangement, stay reachable during core hours, protect company information, and keep their home address and work location current in Workday.
- **Managers** set clear expectations for their team, approve the third in-office day and short-term exceptions, initiate arrangement change requests in Workday, and apply this policy consistently.
- **Vice Presidents** approve or decline Remote arrangement requests and perform the annual review of Remote arrangements in their organization.
- **HR** gives final sign-off on Remote arrangements, approves out-of-state and international work requests, maintains the list of prohibited countries, and answers questions about this policy.
- **Finance** processes stipend and internet claims in SAP Concur under FIN-001.
- **Security** sets the technical controls described in Section 9 and SEC-001.

## 11. Compliance

Failure to follow this policy, for example repeatedly missing anchor days without explanation, working abroad without approval, or connecting to company systems without the VPN, may lead to loss of the Hybrid or Remote arrangement and to disciplinary action under HR-001 Employee Handbook & Code of Conduct. Concerns about how this policy is applied can be raised with a manager, with HR at hr@lumora.example, or anonymously through the Ethics & Compliance hotline at 1-800-555-0142.

## 12. Frequently asked questions

### Q1. What are the required office days for Hybrid employees?

Hybrid employees work in the office 3 days per week. Tuesday and Wednesday are required anchor days, and you choose one additional day with your manager.

### Q2. Who approves a fully Remote arrangement?

Your VP must approve it, and HR must sign off. Remote arrangements are reviewed annually.

### Q3. How much is the home-office stipend, and when must I use it?

Hybrid and Remote employees receive a one-time $500 equipment stipend, which must be used within the first 90 days of employment (or of moving into a Hybrid or Remote arrangement). They can also claim $50 per month for home internet through SAP Concur.

### Q4. Can I work from another country while visiting family?

Yes, for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with approval from your manager and HR requested at least 10 business days in advance. Work from sanctioned countries is never allowed.

### Q5. I want to spend six weeks working from my parents' home in another state. Do I need approval?

Yes. Working from another US state for more than 30 days requires HR approval because of tax and payroll registration. Submit a Temporary Work Location request in Workday.

### Q6. Do I need the VPN when I work from home?

Yes. GlobalProtect VPN is required on any network other than the office network, including your home network. Public Wi-Fi may be used only through the VPN.

### Q7. What hours do I need to be available?

You must be reachable during core collaboration hours, 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time. Agree on the rest of your schedule with your manager.

### Q8. I am on PTO on Tuesday. Do I still need to come in three days that week?

No. Each day of approved PTO or company holiday in a week reduces the in-office requirement by one day, so you would be expected in the office two days that week.
